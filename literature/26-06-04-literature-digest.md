## 🤖 Models that could join the Hub

### 🎯 Activity prediction

- [Clarke et al., *J Chem Inf Model*, 2026-05-28](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.5c03109) 🤖 — **The Tsetlin Machine: A "Third Way" in QSAR Modeling.** Open-source activity-prediction model taking SMILES → bioactivity label (interpretable propositional-logic classifier); released with reference implementation under an open licence by the Molecular Design Group (Dublin City University / TU Dublin). Plausible Hub addition because the propositional output gives human-readable SAR rules, complementing the Hub's existing black-box QSAR endpoints. · 🎯
- [Liu and Chen, *PLoS Comput Biol*, 2026-06-02](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1014369) 🤖 — **PepAnno: A structure-aware deep learning framework for bioactive peptide prediction, structural visualization, and physicochemical profiling.** Open-source activity-prediction model taking peptide SMILES/sequence → multi-label bioactivity + structural visualisation; released as a unified framework from Zhejiang University. Plausible Hub addition because the structure-aware peptide stack closes a known Hub gap between the existing AMP-MIC predictors and richer multi-property peptide screening (input: peptide sequence). · 🎯
- [Wang and Cao, *J Biomed Inform*, 2026-06-01](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbi.2026.105061) 🤖💻 — **IHGCN-PLA: An interpretable heterogeneous graph convolutional network for protein–ligand binding affinity prediction with multimodal interaction fusion.** Open-source CPI activity-prediction model taking SMILES + protein → binding-affinity score (regression) with attention-based interpretability; released with reference implementation. Plausible Hub addition because the SMILES-primary CPI interface fits the Hub's small-molecule input gate and complements the existing docking-surrogate coverage. · 🎯
- [Agha Babaie and Ghasemi, *J Biomol Struct Dyn*, 2026-05-28](https://doi.org/10.1080/07391102.2026.2678411) 🌍🤖 — **Machine learning-driven drug repurposing for GPR17: activity prediction via graph neural networks and multistage computational validation.** LMIC-led activity-prediction model taking SMILES → GPR17 ligand likelihood, from the University of Tehran. Plausible Hub addition because the GPR17 endpoint expands neuro-immunology repurposing coverage that the Hub's antimicrobial-heavy catalogue currently lacks (weights: pending). · 🎯

### 🧩 Featurization

- [Padi and Keyrouz, *bioRxiv*, 2026-06-01](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.05.28.728246) 🤖 — **Species- and Topic-aware Representation Learning for Antimicrobial Peptide Discovery.** Open-source featurization model taking AMP sequence → species- and topic-conditioned embedding, released by the U.S. National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST). Plausible Hub addition because explicit pathogen-conditioned AMP embeddings are missing from the Hub catalogue and would directly feed the molecule-auditing AMR pipeline (input: peptide sequence; weights: pending). · 🧩

### 🎨 Generative

- [Montes-Grajales et al., *Mol Inf*, 2026-04-29](https://doi.org/10.1002/minf.70036) 🤖 — **ChemBang: Expanding the Chemical Space Around Small Molecules.** Open-source generative engine taking SMILES → SMILES via matched-molecular-pair-transformations mined from ChEMBL, released by Chemotargets / Jordi Mestres' group in Barcelona. Plausible Hub addition because MMP-guided expansion is a long-requested companion to the existing scaffold-hopping generators in the Hub. · 🎨
- [Du and Zhong, *J Chem Theory Comput*, 2026-06-03](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.5c02183) 🤖 — **Geometric Structure-Aware Diffusion Model with Self-Optimization Strategy for Molecular Generation.** Open-source generative model taking conditioning vector → 3D SMILES via geometry-aware diffusion with self-optimisation, from Guangxi University. Plausible Hub addition because the geometry-explicit output complements the Hub's existing 2D-only generators and benchmarks against ETFlow / ShEPhERD on the same conformer-quality axis (weights: pending). · 🎨
- [Yu and Pu, *Adv Sci*, 2026-06-02](https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.75928) ⭐🤖 — **De Novo Design of Membrane-Targeting Antimicrobial Peptides Against Gram-Negative Bacteria Using a Generative Artificial Intelligence Framework.** Generative AMP model taking pathogen-condition vector → peptide sequence with mechanism-guided multi-constraint decoding, from South China University of Technology. Plausible Hub addition because Gram-negative-targeted AMP generation is a top Mission-A priority that complements the COMPASS-style activity classifiers (input: peptide sequence; weights: pending). · 🎨
- [Wang and Suessmuth, *bioRxiv*, 2026-06-02](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.05.29.728379) 🤖💻 — **PepForge: Hierarchical HELM-Based Peptide Generation.** Open-source generative model taking HELM macromolecule scaffold → modified peptide (macrocycles, non-natural residues), from Technische Universität Berlin; reference implementation released. Plausible Hub addition because the HELM input opens cyclic/modified peptide chemical space that the Hub's SMILES-only generators cannot currently reach (input: HELM string — Hub-eligible via SMILES export). · 🎨
- [Castanon and Falciani, *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-29](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.04.28.721185) 🤖 — **A Conditional Variational Autoencoder with QSAR-Guided Surrogate-Weighted Fine-Tuning and Cross-Entropy Optimization for Targeted Antimicrobial Peptide Generation.** Conditional VAE taking species + activity-target → AMP sequence, with QSAR-surrogate fine-tuning, from the University of Stavanger. Plausible Hub addition because the surrogate-guided generator pairs naturally with the AMP-SAR featurizer above to form a closed-loop design stack (input: peptide sequence; weights: pending). · 🎨

## AI/ML methods for drug discovery

- [Zhao et al., *Nat Mach Intell*, 2026-05-25](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-026-01234-8) ⭐ — **A large-scale unified deep learning model for peptide mass spectrum interpretation trained on multimodal data.** Multimodal foundation model for peptide MS interpretation from the Chi group; high-impact but not directly Hub-incorporable as small-molecule input (input: peptide + MS spectrum).
- [Li et al., *Nat Chem Biol*, 2026-05-05](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41589-026-02216-y) ⭐ — **Posttranslational modifications remodel proteome-wide ligandability.** Chemoproteomics paper from the Parker lab quantifying how PTMs reshape covalent-ligand binding sites — important context for any Hub generator targeting reactive cysteines, but the analysis runs over protein structures not small molecules (input: protein structure + PTM annotations).
- [Sidhom and Shah, *bioRxiv*, 2026-06-01](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.05.27.728319) — **A Foundation Model for the Cancer Genome.** Weill Cornell foundation model trained on consortium-scale tumour genomes for clinical interpretation; method is interesting but input is genome variants not small molecules (input: genome variants).
- [Wang et al., *J Cheminform*, 2026-06-03](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13321-026-00928-4) — **Assessing the factors influencing the quality of pocket-conditioned 3D generative models.** Systematic evaluation from the Janet / Tibo group at AstraZeneca of what actually makes pocket-conditioned 3D generators work — useful benchmarking context, gated out of 🤖 because the conditioning is on pocket geometry (input: protein pocket).
- [Xiong and Tian, *Digital Discovery*, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1039/d6dd00055j) — **Reaction center prediction by analyzing attention of a chemical language model (PABA/SPABA).** Chemical language model attention interpretability for reaction-centre prediction from Tsinghua / Peking Union Medical, with the 7_7 attention head reaching MCC 0.73 over supervised baselines — relevant retrosynthesis context outside the Hub's current task taxonomy.
- [Cook et al., *Sci Rep*, 2026-04-03](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-44976-6) — **A diffusion model conditioned on compound bioactivity profiles for generating high-content images.** Bioactivity-conditioned image generator from the Godinez group (Novartis) — illustrates one direction for translating Hub-style activity profiles back into phenotypic readouts, gated out of 🤖 because the output is microscopy images (output: HCI images).

## Antibiotic and antimicrobial discovery

- [Shaik and Venkatesan, *J Pept Sci*, 2026-06-01](https://doi.org/10.1002/psc.70098) 🌍 — **Rational Design and Biological Evaluation of Imidazole-Based Tetrapeptides as Antibacterial Leads Against Staphylococcus aureus.** 125 imidazole-based tetrapeptides designed against PBP2a and evaluated *in silico* + experimentally by an LMIC-led team, surfacing a small but reproducible hit series against MRSA/VRSA worth a follow-up Hub-prioritisation pass.
- [Sebti et al., *ChemistryOpen*, 2026-06-01](https://doi.org/10.1002/open.70237) 🌍 — **Assessment of Antimicrobial Activity, Membrane Integrity, and Oxidative Stress of Green Synthesised Silver Nanoparticles.** LMIC-led nano-AMR work using *Acacia karroo* leaf extracts to make AgNPs with sub-100 nm diameters and membrane-disrupting MIC against ESKAPE pathogens — a useful natural-product anchor for the GC-ADDA partner network.
- [Singh and Anvikar, *Discover Nano*, 2026-06-01](https://doi.org/10.1186/s11671-026-04695-3) 🌍 — **Ligand-decorated nanostructure carrier systems for targeted delivery of antimalarials.** ICMR-National Institute of Malaria Research (India) review of artemisinin-derivative delivery vehicles addressing the half-life and bioavailability gaps that limit the current frontline antimalarial regimens.
- [Akinyemi and Ojurongbe, *Sci Rep*, 2026-05-29](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-55249-7) 🌍 — **In-silico identification of a novel *P. falciparum* DHODH inhibitor from *Blighia sapida*.** Nigerian-led GC-MS + docking workflow on *B. sapida* ethanolic leaf extracts, identifying a candidate DHODH inhibitor with relevance to the existing Hub's antimalarial coverage.
- [Alotaiq and Chtita, *MicrobiologyOpen*, 2026-06-01](https://doi.org/10.1002/mbo3.70299) 🌍 — **Targeting MERS-CoV Spike Fusion Machinery with Antiviral Peptides.** LMIC-led (Morocco) *in silico* screen of antiviral peptides against the heptad-repeat 2 domain of the MERS-CoV spike, contributing a small pandemic-preparedness peptide panel to the broader antiviral discovery pipeline.

## AI agents and foundation models for science

- [Pun et al., *Nat Rev Drug Discov*, 2026-04-20](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41573-026-01412-8) ⭐ — **Target identification and assessment in the era of AI.** Insilico Medicine / Zhavoronkov review of how AI agents and foundation models are reshaping drug-target identification — the closest thing this week to the agentic-AI thread the team has been tracking, and a useful frame for benchmarking any future Ersilia AI-assisted target-ID workflow against a published reference architecture.

## Global health and open science

- [Galiwango et al., *Commun Med*, 2026-06-02](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43856-026-01706-3) 🌍 — **Potential of machine learning for prevention and control of neglected tropical diseases: a scoping review.** African Centre of Excellence in Bioinformatics (Uganda) and African Population and Health Research Centre (Kenya) scoping the current ML-for-NTD landscape — a candid map of what is being modelled, where the LMIC-led work sits, and where the gaps are; directly informs the GC-ADDA and EDCTP3 collaboration agenda.
