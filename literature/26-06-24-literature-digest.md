## AI agents and foundation models for science

- [Chawla et al., *Chemical Reviews*, 2026-06-17](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.chemrev.5c01081) — **Organic Chemistry as a Catalyst for AI Innovation: Challenges, Methods, and Emerging Paradigms.** Broad review mapping how organic-chemistry problems have driven ML methods (representation, retrosynthesis, generative design); a useful frame for deciding which method families are worth tracking for the Ersilia Model Hub.
- [Gangwal et al., *Med Res Rev*, 2026-06-17](https://doi.org/10.1002/med.70074) 🌍 — **AI-Driven Synthesis in Medicinal Chemistry: Integrating LLMs, Robotic Automation, and Sustainability Metrics.** India-led review of how language models and lab automation are being wired into synthesis planning; relevant context for where agentic tooling could plug into open drug-discovery workflows.
- [Jones et al., *arXiv*, 2026-06-16](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07022) 💻 — **Self-Driving Datasets: From 20 Million Papers to Nuanced Biomedical Knowledge at Scale (Starling).** LLM-based entity extraction over 22.5M PubMed papers yields open, evidence-linked records on drug properties and gene–disease links — a template for building open knowledge resources behind Hub annotations. · [code](https://github.com/starling-labs/starling)
- [Jiang et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-06-23](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.06.07.730684v1) — **HoloCell: A Generative Foundation Model for Holistic Cellular Modeling.** Foundation-model approach to whole-cell state prediction; out of the Hub's small-molecule-input surface, but worth watching as biological foundation models mature toward phenotype-aware screening.

## AI/ML methods for drug discovery

- [Boiko et al., *Nat Mach Intell*, 2025-05-23](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-025-01031-9) ⭐🤖💻 — **Advancing molecular machine learning representations with stereoelectronics-infused molecular graphs.** Open featurizer that injects stereoelectronic information into molecular graphs and improves downstream property models; a strong drop-in descriptor candidate for the Ersilia Model Hub. · [code](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-025-01031-9#code-availability) · 🧩
- [Wang et al., *Nat Commun*, 2025-09-30](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-63730-6) 🤖 — **Unified and explainable molecular representation learning for imperfectly annotated data (OmniMol).** Hypergraph-view encoder that handles sparse/partial labels across multiple endpoints and stays interpretable — exactly the multi-task featurization profile that dominates Hub incorporations. · 🧩
- [Zhang et al., *J Cheminform*, 2026-06-20](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13321-026-01244-z) 🤖 — **DCPM-ADMET: fusing a dual-component pre-trained model with molecular fingerprints for ADMET prediction.** Combines an XLNet-style sequence module with fingerprints to lift ADMET accuracy under data scarcity; a ready activity/property model in the Hub's single most-incorporated venue. · 🧪
- [Wang et al., *J Chem Inf Model*, 2026-06-18](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c00702) 🤖 — **PAIRMAP: A Unified Geometry-Aware Pairwise-Map Framework for Molecular Representation Learning.** Geometry-aware pairwise encoding for molecular property tasks; another small-molecule featurizer in a top Hub-feeder venue. · 🧩
- [Persico et al., *J Med Chem*, 2026-06-19](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jmedchem.6c00231) 🤖 — **Applying Deep-Learning-Driven De Novo Design to Hit Identification: A Case Study on A₂A Adenosine Receptor Antagonists.** End-to-end generative pipeline taken to experimentally tested hits; the generator emits small molecules and is a plausible Hub generative entry. · 🎨
- [Zhang et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-06-18](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.06.15.732231v1) 🤖 — **OakuloidTM: a hybrid structural-ML and 3D liver-on-chip framework for drug-induced liver injury prediction.** Open, leakage-audited DILI predictor with an optional hepatocyte confirmation signal; addresses a toxicity endpoint the Hub actively covers. · 🧪
- [Sun et al., *Sci Adv*, 2026-06-19](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aeg0376) ⭐ — **De novo molecular generation to dual-track lead optimization: "competition" between AI and chemists for TYR inhibitors.** Head-to-head of RL-based generation against expert medicinal chemists through to optimized leads; a clean case study on where generative chemistry adds value over heuristics.
- [Shi et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-06-17](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.06.15.732318v1) — **DesignMaster: A Multi-Conditional Diffusion Framework for Rational PROTAC Design.** Diffusion model for bifunctional degraders feeding the targeted-protein-degradation thread Ersilia tracks; requires target/E3 conditioning beyond the current Hub generator interface, so noted as context rather than a drop-in.
- [Jeliazkova et al., *Chem Res Toxicol*, 2026-06-22](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.chemrestox.6c00065) — **Beyond Heuristics: A Model-Agnostic Framework for Uncertainty Quantification in QSAR via Adaptive Conformal Prediction.** Calibrated, model-agnostic confidence intervals for QSAR predictions; directly relevant to attaching trustworthy uncertainty to Hub model outputs.
- [Klamt et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-06-16](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.06.12.731585v1) 🗃️ — **THEOBROMA: an aggregated open database of 1.13 million natural products with per-compound license auditing and stereochemistry-aware deduplication.** Large, cleanly-licensed natural-product corpus — a reusable reference library for similarity search and natural-product modelling in the Hub. · 🔍

## Antibiotic and antimicrobial discovery

- [Anahtar et al., *Sci Transl Med*, 2026-06-17](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/scitranslmed.ads4699) ⭐🤖 — **Deep learning–enabled discovery of antibiotics effective against *Neisseria gonorrhoeae*.** Deep-learning models augment a 38,650-compound screen to surface narrow-spectrum hits against drug-resistant gonorrhoea; the activity-model + phenotypic-screen pattern maps directly onto Ersilia's AMR pipelines. · 🎯
- [Rawat et al., *Sci Rep*, 2025-12-27](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-04668-z) 🤖 — **Development of machine learning models to identify potentially active compounds against tuberculosis.** Open QSAR models for anti-TB activity; a directly incorporable classifier for the Hub's TB-prioritisation use cases. · 🎯
- [Meijer et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-06-16](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.06.12.731935v1) — **RetroMol: parsing a shared encoding from natural products and their biosynthetic gene clusters.** Links small-molecule natural products to the BGCs that encode them — useful for antibiotic-discovery workflows mining microbial chemistry, though the BGC input sits outside the Hub's small-molecule surface.
- [Lukacs et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-06-22](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.06.22.733866v1) — **Real-world results from a machine-learning-guided phenotypic high-throughput screen for novel antibiotics.** Learning-to-rank models steer an *E. coli* phenotypic screen to maximise inhibition while minimising human-cell cytotoxicity; a candid read on ML-guided screening in practice.
- [Thakur et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-06-23](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.03.09.710429v1) 🌍 — **A novel 2-substituted benzimidazole with potent antimycobacterial activity and synergy with rifampicin.** India-led medicinal-chemistry work on a TB scaffold with a rifampicin-potentiating combination, adjacent to Ersilia's antitubercular interests.
- [Polloni et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-06-20](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.06.16.732639v1) — **Generating antimicrobial peptides via genomic transfer learning.** Transfer-learning generator for AMPs with predicted MIC activity; peptide output keeps it outside the Hub's small-molecule input surface but informs the AMR-discovery landscape.
- [Wang et al., *Microb Pathog*, 2026-06-18](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.micpath.2026.108642) 🌍 — **In silico and in vitro analysis of resistance genes in *Klebsiella pneumoniae* to assess antibiotic efficacy and identify novel targets.** Pakistan-led study on a priority Gram-negative pathogen central to Ersilia's `E-AMR-CC` work, combining resistance-gene analysis with target identification.
- [Mathpal et al., *Sci Rep*, 2026-06-22](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-57846-y) 🌍 — **Computational screening of antimicrobial peptide analogs targeting the AdeB efflux transporter in *Acinetobacter baumannii*.** India-led work on efflux-mediated resistance in an ESKAPE pathogen, relevant to the permeability/efflux questions in Gram-negative AMR.

## Global health and open science

- [Wicht et al., *ACS Chem Biol*, 2026-06-13](https://doi.org/10.1021/acschembio.6c00264) — **The human Chk1 inhibitor CHIR-124 shows multistage activity against *Plasmodium falciparum* via polypharmacological inhibition of *Pf*Ark1 and hemozoin formation.** H3D/UCT-led antimalarial mechanism study from a core Ersilia partner network, characterising a repurposable multistage scaffold.
- [Snyder et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-06-19](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.06.18.733251v1) — **UCB-9721 as a potent inhibitor of MyoA, the essential class XIV myosin of apicomplexan parasites.** A chemical probe against a conserved motor shared by *Plasmodium* and *Toxoplasma*, of interest to antiparasitic discovery across NTDs.
- [Kawashima et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-06-19](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.06.19.733322v1) — **Antiviral activity of anisomycin against chikungunya virus.** Repurposing-style activity finding against a neglected arboviral disease, adding to the antiviral side of Ersilia's NTD interests.
