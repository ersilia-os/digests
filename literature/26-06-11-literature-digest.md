## AI agents and foundation models for science

- [Anthropic, *Anthropic Research*, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/making-claude-a-chemist) — **Making Claude a chemist.** An account of equipping a general LLM with chemistry tools and reasoning; a useful template for the kind of chemistry-aware agent that could sit on top of Ersilia Model Hub endpoints.
- [Anthropic, *Anthropic Research*, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/research/agents-in-biology) — **Agents in biology.** Survey of LLM agents driving biological research workflows; worth dissecting for how autonomous agents might orchestrate open prediction models in global-health pipelines.
- [Vasina et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-06-07](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.06.04.730034v1) — **CascadeMAP: closed-loop optimisation of enzyme cascades with microfluidics and agentic AI.** Autonomous platform pairing high-throughput microfluidics with Bayesian optimisation and an LLM agent to navigate enzyme-cascade parameter spaces; an instructive blueprint for self-driving experimentation adjacent to open-source discovery tooling.
- [Jiang et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-06-11](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.06.07.730684v1) — **HoloCell: a generative foundation model for holistic cellular modelling.** Unifies epigenomic, transcriptomic and proteomic single-cell layers into one generative model robust to missing modalities; out of the Hub's current small-molecule-input surface but a reference point for foundation-model design.

## AI/ML methods for drug discovery

- [de Oliveira et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-06-10](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.02.15.706011v2) 🤖💻 — **MolDeBERTa: a physicochemical- and substructure-informed molecular representation model.** Open foundation model with weights and training data released on Hugging Face that improves on first-generation SMILES transformers across MoleculeNet; a credible drop-in featurizer for the Ersilia Model Hub. · [code](https://github.com/pcdslab/MolDeBERTa) · 🧩
- [Ben Geoffrey et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-29](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.28.728612v1) 🤖 — **DMPKformer: interpretable multimodal deep learning for ADMET prediction.** Fuses physicochemical, structural and pharmacokinetic views to predict ADMET endpoints from structure alone; squarely in the property-prediction class that makes up a fifth of Hub models. · 🧪
- [Reddy et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-06-08](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.06.08.730844v1) 🤖 — **A drug–target specificity foundation model for off-target prediction, repurposing and generative design.** Casts transformer attention as a Boltzmann binding model to predict small-molecule–protein selectivity without first solving 3D structure; the compound-in interface fits the Hub's compound–protein incorporation surface. · 🎯
- [Das et al., *Comput Biol Chem*, 2026-06-06](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compbiolchem.2026.109167) 🌍🤖 — **CoviPredX: bioactivity prediction for small molecules against SARS-CoV-2.** India-led model trained on ~3,700 ChEMBL compounds with IC50 against the SARS-CoV-2 polyprotein 1ab; a ready candidate antiviral activity predictor for the Hub. · 🎯
- [Tomberg & Carson, *Nat Mach Intell*, 2026-06-09](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-026-01251-7) ⭐ — **Navigating molecular OOD-ness.** Perspective on how out-of-distribution molecules quietly break QSAR and ADMET models and how to detect when it happens; directly relevant to setting applicability-domain expectations for Hub predictors.
- [Mata et al., *Nat Commun*, 2026-04-10](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-71641-3) — **Barcode-free chemical screening for membrane-permeable bioactive macrocycles.** A fully synthetic, genetically-unencoded screening platform that surfaces macrocycles with passive permeability; the permeability readouts are the kind of data that could train Hub permeability models.
- [Guo & Ding, *bioRxiv*, 2026-06-08](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.04.29.721568v3) — **Do larger models really win in drug discovery?** Benchmarks molecular foundation models against compact classical-ML and GNN baselines across 26 ADME, toxicity and bioactivity endpoints; a useful reality check for what the Hub actually gains from scaling.
- [Le et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-06-07](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.06.05.730001v2) — **Multi-body atomic-interaction graphs for protein–ligand binding-energy prediction.** A graph-based scoring function that drops the hand-crafted descriptor stack while aiming for better generalisation on PDBbind; relevant to docking-surrogate work.
- [Banerjee et al., *J Comput Aided Mol Des*, 2026-06-08](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10822-026-00854-x) 🌍 — **A round-robin benchmark for aqueous-solubility prediction.** India-led comparison of chemometric, ML and stacking models on a large curated solubility set; informs how a Hub solubility model should be validated.
- [Thombre et al., *Mol Divers*, 2026-06-09](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11030-026-11615-1) 🌍 — **Selecting an optimal QSAR model for liver-toxicity prediction.** India-led, interpretability-focused comparison for predicting drug-induced hepatotoxicity from structure alone; hepatotoxicity is a recurring Hub property-prediction request.
- [Akinnusi et al., *J Comput Aided Mol Des*, 2026-06-08](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10822-026-00853-y) 🌍 — **Scaffold-split ML repurposing for EGFR inhibition.** Nigeria-led workflow combining potency prediction with docking and MD under honest scaffold-split validation; the validation discipline is the transferable lesson for Hub model building.
- [Feller et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-06-05](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.01.06.697994v4) — **Scaling SMILES-based chemical language models for therapeutic peptides.** Pushes chemical language models to handle the large peptide-like SMILES that protein and small-molecule models both miss; interesting for extending featurizers beyond classic small molecules.

## Antibiotic and antimicrobial discovery

- [Kim et al., *ACS Infect Dis*, 2026-06-09](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsinfecdis.6c00004) — **Machine learning on high-throughput profiling data for antibiotic discovery.** Trains ML models on high-content profiling to flag antibacterial candidates and their likely mechanisms; the workflow overlaps Ersilia's AMR prediction interests.
- [Uddin et al., *ACS Infect Dis*, 2026-01-16](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/full/10.1021/acsinfecdis.5c00760) — **Substrate-specificity checkpoints of the MexF multidrug efflux pump in *Pseudomonas aeruginosa*.** Maps which substrates the MexF pump recognises and rejects; efflux and permeability are core determinants for the Gram-negative AMR chemistry Ersilia tracks.
- [Chacha et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-06-08](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.06.08.730784v1) 🌍 — **Natural-product efflux inhibitors restore bedaquiline susceptibility in resistant *M. tuberculosis*.** Kenya-led work identifying putative efflux inhibitors that reverse bedaquiline resistance; a resistance-reversal angle relevant to TB drug-discovery pipelines.
- [Doering et al., *ACS Infect Dis*, 2026-06-02](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acsinfecdis.6c00100) — **CYP450-mediated metabolism of antimycobacterial Nα-aroyl-N-aryl-phenylalanine amides.** Characterises the metabolic liabilities of a promising anti-TB amide series; the kind of DMPK data that feeds metabolism-prediction models.
- [Gong et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-06-10](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.03.29.715093v2) — **Aminopeptidase-activated peptide prodrugs for species-selective targeting of *Pseudomonas aeruginosa*.** Designs prodrugs activated by a bacterial aminopeptidase to confine antimicrobial-peptide activity to the pathogen; a selectivity strategy for hard-to-treat Gram-negative infection.

## Global health and open science

- [Ait Lahcen et al., *Antiviral Res*, 2026-06-09](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.antiviral.2026.106460) 🌍 — **Natural-product Ebola entry inhibitors targeting the viral glycoprotein.** Morocco-led computational-plus-experimental screen for EBOV-GP inhibitors against a high-fatality pathogen with no approved small-molecule therapy; LMIC-led antiviral discovery of clear global-health relevance.
- [Didier Garnham et al., *Antimicrob Agents Chemother*, 2026-06-04](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42240393/) — **New *Trypanosoma cruzi* hits from mining yeast chemogenomic profiling.** Repurposes existing yeast chemogenomic data to surface fresh Chagas-disease leads; a data-reuse approach that fits Ersilia's neglected-disease, open-data ethos.
