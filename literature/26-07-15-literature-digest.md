## AI agents and foundation models for science

- [Huang et al., *Science*, 2026-07-09](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adz4351) ⭐ — **Autonomous biomedical research with an artificial intelligence agent.** An LLM-driven agent that plans, executes and interprets multi-step biomedical experiments end-to-end, benchmarked on real discovery tasks — a concrete blueprint for the kind of agentic workflow an open-source stack could wrap around Ersilia Model Hub predictors.
- [Rosen et al., *Nature*, 2026-07-08](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10689-z) ⭐ — **Universal cell embedding provides a foundation model for cell biology.** A transformer trained across tens of millions of cells yields a transferable single-cell representation; the input is transcriptomic rather than small-molecule, so it sits outside the Hub's current incorporation surface but is worth tracking as the cell-biology analogue of a chemical foundation model.
- [Aureka AI OpenDDE project, *arXiv*, 2026-07-04](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03787) — **Folding, reasoning and scaling with an open-source drug discovery engine (OpenDDE).** An openly released engine chaining all-atom co-folding, reasoning and affinity estimation into one pipeline — a candidate reference architecture for stitching structure prediction to the Hub's downstream predictors.

## AI/ML methods for drug discovery

- [Xu et al., *JACS Au*, 2026-07-07](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacsau.6c00732) 🤖 — **Design of synthesizable PROTACs through a synthesis-constrained generative model and reinforcement learning.** A generative model biased toward routes chemists can actually make, pushing de novo design into the targeted-protein-degradation space relevant to the glueAI programme; small-molecule output makes it a plausible Hub generator. · 🎨
- [Cruz et al. (Chibale), *Eur J Med Chem*, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejmech.2026.119123) 🤖 — **Targeting HsDHODH: shape- and machine-learning-guided discovery of SARS-CoV-2 antivirals.** A shape-plus-ML virtual-screening pipeline from an H3D / AI2050 co-PI's network surfaces host-target antivirals with structural validation; the activity models are the kind of pathogen-prioritisation classifier the Hub routinely incorporates. · 🎯
- [Shen et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-07-09](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.07.06.736742v1) 🤖 — **BBBP_Atlas: unified interpretable modelling of blood–brain-barrier permeability across small molecules and peptides.** A permeability predictor that drops the usual reliance on hand-picked descriptors and fixed conformers, directly incorporable as an ADMET model in the Ersilia Model Hub. · 🧪
- [Thao & Hung, *Sci Rep*, 2026-07-08](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-60814-1) 🌍🤖 — **3Br-MGD: few-shot toxicity prediction with a three-branch deep encoder and meta-learning.** Vietnam-led work tackling the data-scarcity problem that dogs most toxicity endpoints; a compact small-molecule tox predictor well matched to the Hub's property-prediction catalogue. · 🧪
- [Agboola et al., *Toxicol Mech Methods*, 2026-07-12](https://doi.org/10.1080/15376516.2026.2703006) 🌍🤖 — **Combined fingerprints and descriptors let classical ML match deep learning across Tox21.** A Nigerian-led benchmark showing cheap fingerprint-plus-descriptor models rival GNNs on the full Tox21 panel — an easy, reproducible route to Hub-ready toxicity models. · 🧪
- [Cremer et al., *Nat Commun*, 2026-07-06](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-74130-9) — **FLOWR.ROOT: a flow-matching foundation model for structure-aware 3D ligand generation and affinity prediction.** Strong joint generation-plus-scoring, but its pocket-conditioned input falls outside the Hub's small-molecule-only generator interface for now — flagged as a method to revisit when that surface expands.
- [Moesgaard et al., *JCIM*, 2026-07-08](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c01066) — **Modelling the sensitivity of large-scale virtual screening to scoring-function accuracy, artefacts and library composition.** A systematic look at what actually drives hit rate in billion-scale docking campaigns — directly informative for how ultra-large NTD screens should be set up.
- [Yap et al., *J Cheminform*, 2026-07-06](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13321-026-01254-x) — **ScrambleBench: a workflow for comparative assessment of structure-based de novo generative models.** An open evaluation harness exposing how much benchmark leakage inflates generative-model scores — a useful yardstick before trusting any de novo tool for Hub inclusion.
- [Tursunbadalov et al., *arXiv*, 2026-07-07](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06605) — **A quiet failure in calibrated virtual screening: marginal conformal prediction under-covers the minority class.** Shows standard conformal prediction silently under-covers the rare actives that matter most in screening, with a class-conditional fix — a practical reliability lesson for uncertainty estimates on Hub classifiers.

## Antibiotic and antimicrobial discovery

- [Lepori et al., *Nat Microbiol*, 2026-06-30](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41564-026-02412-5) 🤖 — **Machine-learning identification of chemical features for improved outer-membrane permeation in mycobacteria.** Learns which molecular features drive entry into mycobacteria — the central bottleneck in TB and Gram-negative drug discovery — from small-molecule input, making it a high-priority candidate for the Ersilia Model Hub. · 🧪
- [Serrano et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-07-05](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.07.04.736489v1) 🤖 — **Improving generalisability in whole-cell antibiotic discovery through active learning.** Active learning lifts whole-cell hit rates and, crucially, yields models that hold up on out-of-distribution chemistry — the exact failure mode that limits antibiotic activity predictors in the Hub. · 🎯
- [Oms et al., *Science*, 2026-06-25](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.aei4132) ⭐ — **Bacteria share proteins to survive antibiotics.** Mechanistic evidence that bacteria exchange proteins to weather antibiotic exposure, reframing how tolerance spreads through a population and what resistance-aware screening needs to account for.
- [Crysler & de la Fuente-Nunez, *Cell Host Microbe*, 2026-07](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chom.2026.06.007) — **Mining the code of life for new antibiotics.** A concise map of how genome- and AI-driven mining is reopening antibiotic discovery, from a lab central to the AI-antimicrobials field — useful framing for where open models fit.
- [Al-Wahaibi et al., *RSC Med Chem*, 2026-07-13](https://doi.org/10.1039/d6md00043f) 🌍 — **Methylene disalicylic acid derivatives as dual DNA gyrase / topoisomerase IV antibacterials.** Egyptian-led medicinal chemistry on a dual-target scaffold designed to blunt resistance, generating the kind of MIC data that seeds antibacterial activity models.
- [Singh et al., *Bioorg Chem*, 2026-07-09](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bioorg.2026.110239) 🌍 — **Benzilmonoxime-thiocarbohydrazide Schiff bases as anti-tubercular chemotypes.** India-led synthesis of novel TB scaffolds with integrated computational profiling, adjacent to the H3D and Gates TB pipelines Ersilia supports.
- [Haldiya et al., *Microb Pathog*, 2026-07-11](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.micpath.2026.108694) 🌍 — **Curcumin and pyrazole as inhibitors of Klebsiella pneumoniae LuxS quorum sensing.** Computational identification of anti-virulence hits against an ESKAPE priority pathogen central to the E-AMR-CC programme.
- [El-Helw et al., *Sci Rep*, 2026-07-10](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-60094-9) 🌍 — **Pyrazole-based antimicrobial candidates via integrated synthesis and computation.** Egyptian-led pyrazole medicinal chemistry against resistant pathogens, in the same chemotype family as the NIH R21 antimalarial-pyrazole work.

## Global health and open science

- [Ng'etich et al., *Int J Parasitol Drugs Drug Resist*, 2026-07](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijpddr.2026.100658) — **High-throughput phenotypic screening identifies novel antimalarial scaffolds and target-associated chemotypes.** A fresh phenotypic screen against artemisinin-resistant *Plasmodium falciparum* surfacing new-mechanism scaffolds — candidate training data for antimalarial activity models.
- [Saini et al., *RSC Adv*, 2026-07-08](https://doi.org/10.1039/d6ra02824a) 🌍 — **4-Aminoquinoline and benzoxaborole-4-aminoquinoline hybrids against chloroquine-resistant malaria.** India-led synthesis and antiplasmodial evaluation across sensitive and resistant strains with a heme-inhibition mechanism, feeding the antimalarial SAR Ersilia curates.
- [Wallis et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-07-08](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.07.08.737153v1) — **Mutations within and outside the PfFNT pathway drive MMV007839 resistance in *P. falciparum*.** Maps how resistance emerges to an MMV Malaria Box lead, informing which liabilities antimalarial models should learn to flag.
- [Thatikayala & Sahu, *J Comput Aided Mol Des*, 2026-07-08](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10822-026-00884-5) 🌍 — **N2-substituted triazoles as trypanothione reductase inhibitors with anti-leishmanial activity.** India-led work on the validated TryR target combining in silico design, synthesis and assay — directly relevant to Ersilia's kinetoplastid pipelines.
- [Sabt et al., *RSC Adv*, 2026-07-13](https://doi.org/10.1039/d6ra04647a) 🌍 — **Thiazolidinone-isatin hybrids as anti-*Toxoplasma gondii* agents.** Egyptian-led hybrid-scaffold discovery against a neglected parasite whose current therapies are toxic and incomplete.
- [Nagendran et al., *Mol Divers*, 2026-07-13](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11030-026-11647-7) 🌍 — **Similarity-guided virtual screening of DENV-3 RdRp allosteric inhibitors.** India-led ligand-based screening for dengue polymerase inhibitors, a neglected arbovirus of growing burden across LMIC settings.
