## AI agents and foundation models for science
- [Gao et al., *arXiv*, 2026-05](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28655) — **AutoScientists: Self-Organizing Agent Teams for Long-Running Scientific Experimentation.** Self-organizing teams of LLM agents that sustain long-horizon experimentation autonomously; a reference architecture worth dissecting for open-source agentic workflows over the Ersilia AMR and NTD pipelines.
- [Palem et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-29](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.26.727050v1) — **A Multi-Agent RAG Framework for Biomedical Literature Analysis.** Retrieval-augmented multi-agent system for triaging the ~4,000 papers/day indexed on PubMed; the design maps directly onto automating evidence-gathering around Ersilia model endpoints and target dossiers.

## AI/ML methods for drug discovery
- [Simón et al., *J Chem Inf Model*, 2026-05-30](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c00255) 🤖🧪 — **pKa predicting models trained with a tautomer-complete dataset.** Tackles the protonation-state ambiguity that breaks most pKa models; an obvious small-molecule physicochemical predictor for the Ersilia Model Hub. · 🧪
- [Infante et al., *J Chem Inf Model*, 2026-05-29](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c00556) 🤖🧪 — **Unifying pKa and protonation prediction with sequence models.** Folds pKa and protonation-state prediction into one framework; complements Hub ADMET/physchem models with a reusable ionisation predictor. · 🧪
- [Tomarchio et al., *J Chem Inf Model*, 2026-05-29](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c00967) 🤖🔍 — **A reproducible hierarchical virtual-screening framework integrating scaffold-aware ML, ensemble docking and MD.** Demonstrated on IDO1; the scaffold-aware scoring component is incorporable as a Hub similarity/virtual-screening surrogate. · 🔍
- [Clarke et al., *J Chem Inf Model*, 2026-05-28](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.5c03109) 🤖🎯 — **The Tsetlin Machine: a "third way" in QSAR modeling.** Rule-based, interpretable QSAR sitting between linear models and deep nets; a lightweight transparent activity predictor that fits the Hub's QSAR collection. · 🎯
- [Druchok & Rovenchak, *J Chem Inf Model*, 2026-05-26](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c00407) 🌍🤖🎨 — **When trees guide molecules: multiobjective search in de novo drug design.** Decision-tree/Monte-Carlo generative search for selective or polypharmacological profiles; LMIC-led (Lviv, Ukraine) and a candidate generative model for the Hub. · 🎨
- [Vishwakarma et al., *J Comput Aided Mol Des*, 2026-05-25](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10822-026-00816-3) 🤖🧪 — **ADMET-vault: an interactive framework for real-time ADMET prediction and molecular optimization.** The ADMET-AI-style packaging of property prediction plus optimisation is exactly the pattern the Hub incorporates. · 🧪
- [Cremer et al., *Nat Comput Sci*, 2026-05-28](https://doi.org/10.1038/s43588-026-00998-8) — **FLOWR: flow matching for structure-aware de novo, interaction- and fragment-based ligand generation.** High-impact generative method with equivariant optimal transport, though its protein-pocket conditioning sits outside the Hub's current small-molecule-input surface.
- [Wang et al., *Commun Chem*, 2026-05-26](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42004-026-02076-6) 🗃️🧩 — **A dataset of 1.2 million molecules with DFT-level quantum-chemical annotations for molecular representation learning.** A large, openly released, citable corpus for pre-training or benchmarking Hub featurizers. · 🧩
- [Wang et al., *J Cheminform*, 2026-05-30](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13321-026-01230-5) — **Assessing the factors influencing the quality of pocket-conditioned 3D generative models.** Systematic AstraZeneca study of what drives generator quality; useful guidance before trusting any structure-based generator that feeds a screening pipeline.
- [Janisiów et al., *arXiv*, 2026-05-27](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28226) — **PhAME: phenotype-aware molecular editing via latent diffusion.** Similarity-constrained lead optimisation guided by phenotypic signatures; promising, but the phenotype conditioning is not yet suppliable through the Hub's generator interface.

## Antibiotic and antimicrobial discovery
- [Tariq et al., *RSC Adv*, 2026-05-26](https://doi.org/10.1039/d6ra02379g) 🌍🤖🎨 — **Data-driven design and screening of novel Klebsiella pneumoniae carbapenemase-2 inhibitors using a generative chemical language model.** LMIC-led (Karachi, Pakistan) and squarely on an ESKAPE / E-AMR-CC priority pathogen; incorporable as a target-focused generator. · 🎨
- [da Costa et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-31](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.28.726277v1) 🤖🎯 — **Instance-wise contrastive graph neural network enables the discovery of novel Aedes aegypti larvicidal compounds.** A small-molecule activity model for vector-borne-disease chemistry, ready for Hub incorporation. · 🎯
- [Maluf et al., *ACS Infect Dis*, 2026-05-25](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsinfecdis.5c00682) — **Efficacy, structure–activity relationship and mode-of-action of a new generation of acridine/acridone-based antimalarials.** Fresh Plasmodium chemotypes with SAR and MoA data, adjacent to Ersilia's antimalarial prioritisation work.
- [Özsan et al., *chemRxiv*, 2026-05-25](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv.15003837/v1) — **Unlocking iridium chemical space for antibiotic discovery through one-pot combinatorial synthesis in a direct-to-biology approach.** An unconventional source of antibacterial scaffolds worth feeding into screening libraries.
- [Ucar et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-26](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.23.727347v1) — **Pathogen-specific antimicrobial activity prediction with biological large language model-based methods.** Useful AMR/AMP target context, though the peptide-sequence input is outside the Hub's small-molecule surface.
- [Hassan, *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-28](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.24.727537v1) — **Minimal computational framework for systematic identification of antimicrobial targets.** A lean protein-dynamics approach to target discovery that avoids resource-intensive empirical screens — worth tracking for AMR pipelines.
- [Jibrin & Umar, *In Silico Pharmacol*, 2026-05-26](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40203-026-00646-z) — **Design of benzofuro[3,2-d]pyrimidine-based MmpL3 inhibitors as potential anti-tuberculosis agents.** On an Ersilia-priority TB target (MmpL3), albeit still at the docking/SAR stage.

## Global health and open science
- [Weeks et al., *npj Antimicrob Resist*, 2026-05-29](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44259-026-00220-9) — **Addressing the dual-use of antifungals and fungal antimicrobial resistance (fAMR) through a One Health approach.** Frames a neglected AMR axis — agricultural vs clinical antifungal use — relevant to global-health surveillance priorities.
- [Vass et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-30](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.27.728203v1) — **Local temperature and humidity are associated with the proportion of antimicrobial-resistant E. coli in farm environments.** Informs climate-aware design of on-farm AMR surveillance.
- [Kumar et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-28](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.06.18.658431v2) — **Data representation bias and conditional distribution shift drive predictive-performance disparities in multi-population machine learning.** A direct cautionary input for equitable deployment of predictive models across LMIC populations.
