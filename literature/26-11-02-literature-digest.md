## 🤖 Models that could join the Hub

### 🧪 Property prediction

- [Wang et al., *J Chem Inf Model*, 2026-10-27](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c02699) 🤖💻 — **Geometry-Aware Molecular Representation Learning for Approximating NORMAN-Predicted Retention Time Indices in Nontargeted Screening (ViSRTI).** Open-source property-prediction model taking SMILES (3D conformers built internally, plus descriptor columns per the repository) → retention time index for LC-HRMS screening; released with weights and code on GitHub under MIT. Plausible Hub addition as a niche analytical-chemistry property model, but the repository is Windows/CUDA-specific and needs user-supplied descriptors (infra: heavy), and the target is NORMAN-predicted rather than measured values. · [code](https://github.com/WHWH1111/ViSRTI) · 🧪

## 🗃️ Datasets that could join the Hub

### Bioactivity datasets

- [Pirtskhalava et al., *Nucleic Acids Res*, 2026-10-31](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkag1050) 🗃️ — **DBAASP V4: condition-resolved bioactivity and chemical structures for data-driven peptide and peptidomimetic discovery.** More than 25,000 entries (about 4,100 natural and 21,000 synthetic peptides and peptidomimetics) · condition-resolved antimicrobial, antibiofilm, haemolytic and cytotoxic activity · data licence to confirm (article is CC BY) · dbaasp.org bulk CSV/JSON and REST API. Plausible Hub input because canonical SMILES are provided for structurally defined peptides and the activity labels are AMR-relevant. · 🎯
- [Ge et al., *Nucleic Acids Res*, 2026-10-31](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkag1098) 🗃️ — **PROTAC-DB 4.0: an expanded knowledgebase of targeted protein degradation modalities with AI-powered knowledge discovery.** 10,725 unique PROTAC SMILES (15,502 entries) and 15,237 unique molecular-glue SMILES (24,798 entries) · degrader structures, targets, E3 ligases and degradation activity · data licence not stated (article is CC BY-NC; do not assume permissive) · cadd.zju.edu.cn/protacdb. Plausible Hub input because targeted protein degradation is an Ersilia strategic priority and the set could train degradation-activity or E3-ligase models. · 🎯

### Generative training corpora

- [Bosc et al., *Nucleic Acids Res*, 2026-10-27](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkag1057) 🗃️ — **SureChEMBL in 2026: an open database of chemical and biomedical annotations from the patent literature.** 31 million structures extracted from patents, with gene, protein, disease and mechanism annotations · data licence to confirm (article is CC BY 4.0) · surechembl.org bulk download and API. Plausible Hub input as a large pretraining or generative-training corpus. · 🎨

## AI/ML methods for drug discovery

- [*Nature Methods*, 2026-10-30](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-026-03271-y) ⭐ — **Foundation models unlock robust phenotypic representations from Cell Painting images.** Image-based foundation models for phenotypic profiling. (Input: microscopy images, outside the current Hub small-molecule surface; abstract, code and weights were not accessible, so incorporability is unverified.)
- [Tran-Nguyen et al., *Nat Mach Intell*, 2026-10-23](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-026-01331-8) ⭐ — **Guidelines for evaluating structure-based methods for protein–ligand binding prediction.** Evaluation guidelines from Tran-Nguyen and Ballester. Useful reference when benchmarking docking and cofolding tools. (Input: protein–ligand structures.)
- [Ji et al., *Sci Adv*, 2026-10-30](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aei5257) ⭐ — **A Full-Spectrum Generative Lead Discovery (FSGLD) pipeline via DRUG-GAN.** Generative pipeline that links DRUG-GAN models to docking, molecular dynamics, free-energy calculations and experimental validation. (Target-conditioned; code and weights not verified.)
- [Zaliani et al., *J Med Chem*, 2026-10-29](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jmedchem.6c00791) — **Democratizing Drug Discovery: A Scalable Open-Source Pipeline for Massive Specific Protein Target QSAR Modeling Using ChEMBL.** Automated pipeline that built 2,263 target-specific QSAR models from ChEMBL with random forest and XGBoost, filtered by R² and MCC thresholds. Closely matches the Hub's activity-model pattern (one codebase, many targets); the code and model release location could not be verified and needs a manual check.
- [Ghimire et al., *Mol Inform*, 2026-10-29](https://doi.org/10.1002/minf.70060) — **Interpretable Drug–Target Prediction via Geometric Alignment: A Linear Alternative to Deep Architectural Complexity.** Aligns frozen ChemBERTa and ESM-2 embeddings in a shared linear space; reports AUROC 0.936 on KIBA and 0.928 on Davis. (Code and weights not found; human kinase scope.)
- [Saeed et al., *J Mol Biol*, 2026-10](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmb.2026.170075) — **HuKSA: A Machine-Learning-Based Framework for Predicting Human Kinome Selectivity and Polypharmacology.** SMILES-in kinase-target and selectivity read-across over an atlas of 794 compounds, with a hosted web server. (Licence unverified, no code repository found, human targets only.)
- [Linker et al., *ChemMedChem*, 2026-10-30](https://doi.org/10.1002/cmdc.70553) — **Sequence Scanning Improves Accuracy for Antibody–Antigen and Molecular Glue Ternary Complex Cofolding.** Workflow evaluated on Boltz and Protenix that adds systematic constraints to improve sampling diversity for ternary complexes. Relevant to cofolding in targeted protein degradation work.
- [Najdovski et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-10-30](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.10.26.761649) 💻 — **When Does Active Learning Improve ADMET Prediction? A Multi-Endpoint Benchmark.** Compares seven acquisition strategies and two uncertainty methods on eight ADMET regression endpoints under a chemotype split; diversity-aware selection was the most consistent. (No predictive model released; no licence on the repository.) · [code](https://github.com/VikiShmiki/Active-Learning-ADMET)
- [Stamenov et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-10-30](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.10.26.761652) 💻 — **Task-Dependent Performance of GNNs and Pretrained Models in ADMET Prediction.** Benchmarks tuned GNNs, frozen ChemBERTa and MolCLR embeddings, and fingerprints on six TDC ADMET endpoints; no family wins everywhere. (Benchmark of existing methods; licence not confirmed on GitHub.) · [code](https://github.com/NitramVonemats/ADMET-Benchmark)
- [Chrin et al., *ChemRxiv*, 2026-10-29](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv.15011290) — **Comparative Analysis of Basic Machine Learning and Rigorous Free-Energy Calculation on Benchmarking Datasets of Protein-Ligand Binding Affinity.** Compares descriptor-based ML and 3D-QSAR with free-energy calculations on public benchmarks, from ETH Zürich. A reference point for when a cheap Hub activity model is enough.
- [Zhao et al., *IEEE/ACM Trans Comput Biol Bioinform*, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1109/tcbbio.2026.3748139) — **MolCLW: Molecular Contrastive Learning With Learnable Weighted Substructures.** Contrastive graph encoder that learns how much weight each masked substructure receives; reports an average 2.2% ROC-AUC gain on six classification benchmarks. (Featurizer, but no code or weights found.)

## Antibiotic and antimicrobial discovery

- [Hanson et al., *PNAS*, 2026-10-28](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2616439123) — **Prioritize validation over discovery of novel antibiotics.** Argues that validating existing antibiotic candidates should take priority over discovering new ones; a useful framing for how AMR model predictions are ranked for follow-up.
- [Ye et al., *mBio*, 2026-10-30](https://doi.org/10.1128/mbio.01884-26) — **Potent reaction hijacking inhibitors of *Plasmodium falciparum* asparagine tRNA synthetase.** 4-Aminoquinazoline sulfonamides with nanomolar blood-stage potency, activity against transmissible stages and no resistance in culture; targeted mass spectrometry shows reaction hijacking of the synthetase.
- [Gervasoni et al., *J Cheminform*, 2026-10-20](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13321-026-01314-2) — **AB-DB v2.0: an integrated chemoinformatic resource for antimicrobial compounds.** More than 470 molecules across 51 antimicrobial classes with MD/QM-derived descriptors and force-field parameters; no bioactivity labels, and below the Hub dataset threshold.
- [Tang et al., *Nucleic Acids Res*, 2026-10-27](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkag1045) — **Omni-ARMD: an integrated genomic and clinical knowledgebase bridging clinical phenotypes and molecular determinants of antimicrobial resistance.** 7.84 million standardised susceptibility records across 38 organisms and 52 antimicrobials, linked to resistance genes. (No structures; data licence not stated; article is CC BY-NC.)
- [Gupta et al., *Mol Biol Rep*, 2026-10-29](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11033-026-12863-y) — **Functional characterization of PfHDDCP reveals a HotDog-domain acyl-CoA thioesterase as a potential antimalarial drug target.** First biochemical evidence that the *P. falciparum* protein has thioesterase activity, supporting it as a target for blood-stage drug discovery.
- [Nisar et al., *SAR QSAR Environ Res*, 2026-10-27](https://doi.org/10.1080/1062936x.2026.2751659) — **Pretrained graph transformer-driven de novo design and machine learning prioritization of computationally predicted ENR-InhA inhibitors.** Pipeline built on 80 curated ChEMBL InhA IC50 values, using DrugGEN for generation. Small training set; code and data release not seen.
- [Green et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-10-27](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.10.26.760366) — **DDU384: A new preclinical candidate for the treatment of Tuberculosis.** Preclinical tuberculosis candidate from the University of Dundee Drug Discovery Unit; a reference compound for TB whole-cell activity models.
- [Romano et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-10-29](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.10.26.761825) — **Interpreting Graph Neural Networks for Antibacterial Drug Repurposing against *Acinetobacter baumannii*.** A graph neural network ensemble matches a descriptor-augmented reference model on an antibacterial screen using only atom features, and atom-level explanations are used to propose chemical hypotheses. (Activity model on SMILES, but no code or weights seen.)
- [van der Voort et al., *ChemRxiv*, 2026-10-23](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv.15010946) — **A Direct-to-Biology Platform for Rapid Covalent Antibiotic Discovery.** 720 crude chloroacetamides made in days and screened against MRSA without purification; hits were confirmed on resynthesis and chemoproteomics plus crystallography pointed to the target. Relevant to fast design–make–test loops.
- [Chen et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-10-29](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.10.27.762313) — **Engineered probiotics target granulomas to treat mycobacterial infection.** *E. coli* Nissle 1917 engineered to colonise granulomas and release antimycobacterial payloads through a dual-mode lysis circuit.

## AI agents and foundation models for science

- [Born et al., *Digital Discovery*, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1039/d6dd00404k) — **Toward full-cycle automation of closed-loop molecular discovery – a case study on JAK targets.** IBM Research and Arctoris ran automated design–make–test–analyse cycles with generative design, robotic synthesis and cloud assays; the second round improved potency and ligand efficiency. (Depends on proprietary robotic and assay platforms; openness of the workflow not verified.)
- [Ananikov et al., *Chemistry*, 2026-10-27](https://doi.org/10.3390/chemistry8110147) — **Artificial Intelligence and Big Data in Chemistry: From Molecular Data to Autonomous Discovery.** Review of AI and data-driven approaches in chemistry through to autonomous discovery; a broad orientation for automation work.
