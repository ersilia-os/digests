## 🤖 Models that could join the Hub

### 🧪 Property prediction

- [Kretschmer et al., *Nature Methods*, 2026-10-01](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-026-03243-2) ⭐🤖 — **Times are changing but order matters: transferable prediction of small-molecule liquid chromatography retention times.** Open-source property model taking SMILES plus chromatographic-system parameters → a retention order index that anchor compounds map to retention times; a graph network trained on public reversed-phase data, with code reported by the authors' group (repository URL and licence not verified, so 💻 is withheld) (weights: pending). Plausible Hub addition because it extends Property coverage to an analytical-chemistry endpoint useful for metabolite annotation. · 🧪
- [Lee et al., *arXiv*, 2026-09-30](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38744) 🤖💻 — **Molecular Property Prediction under Structural Shift with Tabular Foundation Models (MolPAIR).** Open-source property-prediction model taking SMILES → property value; released with code on GitHub (code licence not stated; the CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 on the arXiv page covers the text) and no training of its own, since it queries two frozen tabular foundation models in context (infra: depends on those checkpoints). Plausible Hub addition because it targets scaffold-shifted test sets, where fixed-endpoint Hub property models degrade first. · [code](https://github.com/nums-ai/MolPAIR) · 🧪

### 🧩 Featurization

- [Mo et al., *arXiv*, 2026-09-29](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37384) 🤖💻 — **MoTIF-X: a multimodal tokenized framework for interpretable and extensible molecular representation learning.** Open-source featurization model taking SMILES (with graph and 3D conformer views) → motif-anchored embeddings that also drive property and drug-target prediction; code on GitHub (licence not stated; paper CC BY 4.0) (weights: pending; infra: needs 3D conformers). Plausible Hub addition because substructure-level interpretability is rare among Hub featurizers. · [code](https://github.com/Bin-Chen-Lab/Motif-X) · 🧩

### 🎨 Generative

- [Mazuz et al., *Bioinformatics*, 2026-10-01](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btag731) 🤖💻 — **Multi-Objective-Conditioned-Return-Blocks Decision Transformer (M-GoRT) for multi-objective de novo drug design.** Open-source generative model taking target property objectives (e.g. DRD2, JNK3) → SMILES; released with code under MIT on GitHub (weights: pending). Plausible Hub addition because multi-objective conditioning with high diversity and novelty would give the Hub a controllable sampler beyond single-property generators. · [code](https://github.com/eyalmazuz/MolGen/tree/GCRB-DT) · 🎨

## 🗃️ Datasets that could join the Hub

### Bioactivity datasets

- [Kumar et al., *arXiv*, 2026-09-24](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29740) 🗃️💻 — **TopU-LBVS: a realistic multi-target benchmark for ligand-based virtual screening.** 93 protein targets across 7 classes · roughly 0.4–10k compounds per target library with hard negatives · CC-BY-4.0 · GitHub and Hugging Face. Plausible Hub input because a leakage-aware activity and similarity benchmark is what Hub models lack; total row count is not stated, so it is promoted at editorial discretion. · [code](https://github.com/topu-benchmark/topu-lbvs) · 🎯

## AI/ML methods for drug discovery

- [Cao et al., *Nature Communications*, 2026-10-01](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-78105-8) — **Evaluating generative models for structure-based drug discovery from de novo design to lead optimization.** Benchmarks pocket-conditioned generators beyond standard metrics, including chemical safety and scaffold diversity. (Input: protein pocket — Hub-eligible once protein-input support lands.)
- [Grisoni, *arXiv*, 2026-09-29](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.37550) — **How "foundational" are current molecular foundation models?** Proposes generality, transferability and out-of-distribution generalisation as tests; none of the models examined meets all three, and data quality matters more than scale. Essential reading before adding another pretrained encoder to the Hub.
- [Yim, *J Chem Inf Model*, 2026-09-30](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c02441) — **Fréchet ChemNet Distance is blind to double-bond stereochemistry.** Shows a standard generative-model metric cannot separate E/Z isomers; relevant to any evaluation of Hub generators.
- [Chittick et al., *J Med Chem*, 2026-09-30](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jmedchem.6c01283) — **Summarizing replicate data from medicinal chemistry assays.** Practical guidance on aggregating repeat measurements; bears directly on curating Hub training sets from ChEMBL-style data.
- [Menzeleev et al., *Digital Discovery*, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1039/d6dd00162a) — **Understanding machine learning models trained on DNA-encoded libraries for virtual screening.** Probes what DEL-trained models actually learn; useful before treating DEL-derived activity models as Hub candidates.
- [Truong et al., *ChemRxiv*, 2026-09-30](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv.15009691/v1) — **Small-molecule data splitting for machine learning in drug design.** Reviews splitting strategies for evaluation, tuning and feature selection; a reference for the Hub's validation protocols.
- [Elshewy, *J Chem Inf Model*, 2026-09-16](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c02895) — **The synthesizability gap has moved: route existence is not chemistry.** Proposes a cheap chemistry-validity layer for scoring synthesis routes, a caution for generator outputs.
- [Wang et al., *Trends Pharmacol Sci*, 2026-10](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tips.2026.08.006) — **AI virtual cells for drug discovery and pharmacology.** Review of cell-state foundation models as a route from compound to phenotype.
- [Käll et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-09-29](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.09.23.753795) — **Assessing chemical toxicity across Eukaryota using multimodal transformers (TRIDENT-2).** Predicts toxicity from structure, species and exposure conditions over 560,780 assays under CC-BY 4.0; code and weights links not stated, and species input goes beyond a plain SMILES interface.
- [Jiang et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-10-02](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.09.27.754850) — **Pan-screening the structural predictability of drug toxicity: validation of molecular fingerprints.** 1,120 drugs against 768 adverse-reaction endpoints; compact 166-bit fingerprints match 2,048-bit ones and yield structural alerts. No code link; the corpus is under the 10k-row dataset bar.
- [Qiao et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-09-21](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.09.15.751932) — **Reaction-aware hypergraph learning of chemical representations with LARK.** Pretrains on about 0.6 million reactions and is evaluated on 42 tasks; (license: CC-BY-NC) and no code linked, so not Hub-eligible.
- [Huang et al., *arXiv*, 2026-10-01](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02186) — **Higher-order molecular grammars for generative and foundation models in chemistry.** Encodes ring systems as grammar production rules, giving 100% validity in generation; no code or weights yet, so a watch item for a generative or featurization entry.
- [Song et al., *arXiv*, 2026-09-29](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38484) — **RetroGEF: dynamic graph edit flow for single-step retrosynthesis.** Flow-based reactant generation by atom and bond edits; no code link on the abstract page.
- [Sulu et al., *arXiv*, 2026-08-30](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.29886) — **Structural hierarchy and geometry in molecular representation learning.** Shows Bemis–Murcko scaffold supervision organises embeddings by scaffold in Euclidean and Lorentz spaces; no code link, relevant to Hub projection work.

## Antibiotic and antimicrobial discovery

- [Safari et al., *J Am Chem Soc*, 2026-09-27](https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.6c08350) — **Siderophore-enabled delivery of carbapenem and metallo-β-lactamase inhibitor: a promising strategy against multidrug-resistant *Pseudomonas aeruginosa*.** A Trojan-horse conjugate aimed at carbapenem-resistant *P. aeruginosa*, a WHO priority pathogen with poor outer-membrane uptake.
- [You et al., *Research*, 2026-09-30](https://doi.org/10.34133/research.1457) — **Artificial intelligence as multifaceted toolkit for antibacterial drug discovery against resistance.** Review mapping where GNNs, descriptor QSAR and generative models fit antibacterial discovery; a map of the Hub's AMR modelling gaps.
- [Kurosu et al., *Expert Opin Drug Discov*, 2026-09-27](https://doi.org/10.1080/17460441.2026.2736532) — **Strategy-based design for next-generation β-lactam antibiotics that evade resistance in Gram-negative pathogens.** Review of the β-lactam design landscape.
- [Moescheid et al., *Science Advances*, 2026-10-02](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aeh0455) ⭐ — **Toward transgenic multicellular parasites: CRISPR-Cas12a germline editing of *Schistosoma mansoni* eggs.** Heritable editing opens target validation in a neglected-disease parasite.
- [Salim et al., *Antimicrob Agents Chemother*, 2026-10-01](https://doi.org/10.1128/aac.00144-26) — **G-quadruplex-binding ligand RHPS4 reduces *Plasmodium falciparum* mitochondrial DNA level, providing a novel antimalarial strategy.** A mechanism distinct from current antimalarials, relevant to resistance-breaking chemotypes.
- [Van Truong et al., *Antimicrob Agents Chemother*, 2026-09-30](https://doi.org/10.1128/aac.00382-26) — **Anti-plasmodial activity of proteasome inhibitors against various *Plasmodium* species.** Preclinical evaluation of ONX-0914-class proteasome inhibitors across species.
- [Zorawik et al., *J Clin Microbiol*, 2026-09-30](https://doi.org/10.1128/jcm.00598-26) — **Rifabutin disk diffusion on chelated Mueller-Hinton agar for *Acinetobacter baumannii* susceptibility testing.** A methods framework for repurposing rifabutin against carbapenem-resistant *A. baumannii*.
- [Wani et al., *Drug Discov Today*, 2026-10](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.drudis.2026.104823) — **Revisiting the potential of elongation factors as drug targets for pathogenic mycobacteria.** Review of translation-factor targets for TB and non-tuberculous mycobacteria.
- [Dhindwal et al., *ACS Chem Biol*, 2026-10-05](https://doi.org/10.1021/acschembio.6c00562) — **Ebselen inactivates essential *Mycoplasmopsis bovis* proteins.** A roughly 2,800-compound screen finds covalent inhibition of three enzymes at poorly conserved cysteines, a route to narrow-spectrum agents.
- [Rey-Sogo et al., *Environ Sci Technol*, 2026-10-02](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.6c02828) — **Predicting chemical stressor effects on conjugative plasmid transfer rates using machine learning.** Morgan-fingerprint models trained on 73 studies predict how compounds modulate RP4 plasmid transfer (best external Q² about 0.37); no code link found, so not a Hub candidate yet.
- [Hsiao et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-09-29](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.09.28.755224) — **De novo minibinders targeting RH5 achieve nanomolar inhibition of blood-stage malaria replication.** Designed protein binders against the essential RH5–basigin invasion step.
- [Zingl et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-09-30](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.09.29.755485) — **Antibody-recruiting molecules based on mimotope fusion peptides and a dimeric antibiotic.** Aims to recruit host antibodies against multidrug-resistant Gram-negative bacteria.
- [Tsai et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-10-01](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.09.24.754085) — **Branch-wise regularization for heterogeneous GNN-based MALDI-TOF AMR prediction with biomarker-consensus edges.** The graph helps only conditionally on the data; the biomarker features agree with known oxacillin markers (input: mass spectra, so not Hub-eligible).
- [Rodden et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-09-29](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.09.27.754834) — **Bacteriophage and antibiotic resistance are positively associated across clinical *Pseudomonas aeruginosa* isolates.** Cautions that phage-antibiotic co-therapy may face correlated resistance.

## AI agents and foundation models for science

- [Stutz et al., *Nature*, 2026-09-30](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10965-y) ⭐ — **Function-preserving watermarking of AI-generated proteins.** An identifiable pattern is woven into designed sequences without loss of function; a biosecurity and provenance layer for generative biology.
- [Fang et al., *arXiv*, 2026-10-01](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00898) — **When do biological reasoning models use their biological inputs?** Across six models, foundation-model inputs matter for ChatNT, Prot2Text-V2 and CellWhisperer but barely for Evo2 or ESM3; a check to run before trusting multimodal science agents.
- [Rendy et al., *arXiv*, 2026-09-24](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29344) — **Combining physical models with dynamically acquired experimental information for optimisation of NASICON conductors in a self-driving laboratory.** Cost-guided autonomous synthesis found 18 promising compositions in 78 trials; a closed-loop template for chemistry automation.
- [Li et al., *arXiv*, 2026-09-30](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39060) — **Autocata: reaction-network-guided autonomous catalyst screening via capability-centric AI.** An agent wrapping 79 specialised generative models behind reusable capabilities, a tool-first agent design; no code link.

## Global health and open science

- [Cumming et al., *Science Advances*, 2026-10-02](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aei0953) ⭐ — **How climate change affects mosquito-borne infectious disease: the different trajectories of malaria and dengue.** Review separating malaria and dengue responses to warming, framing where antimalarial and antiviral demand will shift.
- [Abdi et al., *Global Public Health*, 2026-10-02](https://doi.org/10.1080/17441692.2026.2742514) — **Antimicrobial resistance and social inequity: why the One Health approach must centre poverty.** Argues AMR policy under-weights poverty as a driver of the 4.71 million associated deaths.
- [Nguyen et al., *Front Public Health*, 2026-10-01](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2026.1965508) — **From discovery to preparedness: reframing antimicrobial resistance as a global governance challenge.** Shifts emphasis from discovery to stewardship and surveillance and calls for decoupling antibiotic revenue from sales volume.
- [Hayato et al., *BMC Pediatrics*, 2026-10-03](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12887-026-07748-9) — **Bacterial profile and antimicrobial resistance patterns among paediatric intensive care unit patients in Ethiopia: a retrospective cohort study, 2020–2025.** Local resistance data from an Ethiopian PICU, the kind of surveillance that grounds pathogen priorities for LMIC-relevant models.
- [Okoduwa et al., *ChemClass Journal*, 2026-09-30](https://doi.org/10.67867/ccj.2026v10i3.10) 🌍 — **Phytochemicals targeting diabetes and malaria: chemical diversity, mechanistic insights and prospects for dual-action therapeutics.** Nigeria-led review of plant chemotypes with antimalarial activity, a source of scaffolds for tropical-disease screening.
