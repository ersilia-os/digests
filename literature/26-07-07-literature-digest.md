## AI agents and foundation models for science
- [Gao et al. (Zitnik), *arXiv*, 2026-06-27](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28692) — **ATHENA-R1: an AI agent for treatment reasoning over a biomedical tool universe.** A reinforcement-learning agent that reasons over FDA-approved drugs and biomedical tools and grounds each therapy choice in verifiable sources; a concrete, tool-grounded scientific-agent design worth dissecting for an open reimplementation.
- [Pareja-Lorente et al. (Aloy), *bioRxiv*, 2026-07-05](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.06.30.735584) — **Task-adapted biological foundation models uncover perturbation-centric representations.** From the Chemical Checker group: refocusing a scGPT-style model from cell-state to perturbation effects by changing the training objective, informing how bioactivity representations are learned — though the transcriptomic input sits outside the Hub's small-molecule surface.

## AI/ML methods for drug discovery
- [Zhang & Lapkin, *Nat Commun*, 2026-06-27](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-75005-9) 🤖 — **Dual graph transformer (DGT) for molecular property prediction.** Self-attention encoder that jointly models atom and bond graphs plus stereogeometry for richer molecular encodings; a general-purpose featurizer that could slot into the Hub. · 🧩
- [Masud et al., *J Chem Inf Model*, 2026-07-03](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c01010) 🤖 — **QSAR in the Browser (QITB).** A client-side static web app that runs QSAR with no programming and no data upload, preserving privacy; a lightweight distributable-tool pattern that fits the Hub's web-tool incorporation route. · 🎯
- [Peng et al., *J Biomed Inform*, 2026-07-02](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbi.2026.105076) 🤖 — **CoAff-DTI: fine-grained drug–target interaction prediction.** Pairs pretrained language models with an affinity-guided module to resolve substructure–binding-site interactions; a compound-primary DTI model of the kind the Hub already hosts. · 🎯
- [Getz et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-07-06](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.07.04.736485) 🤖 — **BoltzMol-1: reliable virtual screening for hit discovery.** A small-molecule hit-finding pipeline built on an optimised Boltz-2 that aims to generalise beyond well-characterised target families; tracks the team's ongoing Boltz-2 cofolding interest. · 🔍
- [Mansour & Rafaelyan, *bioRxiv*, 2026-07-05](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.06.30.735582) 🤖 — **ADMET property prediction with quantum-inspired preprocessing.** Encodes descriptor dependencies into a parameterised many-body Hamiltonian ahead of standard ADMET models; an ADMET predictor in the Hub's single largest incorporation category. · 🧪
- [Wang et al. (Coley), *arXiv*, 2026-06-28](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.29161) 🤖💻 — **GLACIER: mass-spectrum prediction as object detection.** Reframes MS/MS prediction from molecular structure as peak detection, with open code from the Coley group; a small-molecule-in property predictor useful for metabolomics-adjacent Hub tasks. · 🧪 · [code](https://github.com/coleygroup/ms-pred)
- [Martínez Galindo et al., *Nat Commun*, 2026-06-29](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-74487-x) — **Impact of molecular multimodality on neural-network models for drug discovery.** Systematic benchmark of fusing seven molecular modalities across architectures and fusion strategies; a useful baseline for deciding when multimodal featurizers actually beat single-modality ones.
- [Huber et al., *J Cheminform*, 2026-06-30](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13321-026-01245-y) — **Count your bits: fingerprint benchmarking across chemical space.** Broad, systematic comparison of fingerprint types, binary-vs-count representation and folding on Tanimoto behaviour; a practical reference for the similarity-search and ML-evaluation choices underpinning Hub descriptors.
- [Nazarova et al., *npj Drug Discov*, 2026-07-03](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44386-026-00053-6) — **V-SYNTHES2: next-generation giga-scale structure-based virtual screening.** Extends modular synthon-based screening of on-demand chemical spaces with >1000× speedups over brute-force docking; relevant to ultra-large VS even though its target-structure input sits outside the Hub's small-molecule surface.
- [Guan et al., *arXiv*, 2026-06-30](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31126) — **Can tabular in-context learners generalise to biomolecular property prediction?** Tests TabPFN/TabICL-style foundation models as few-shot predictors on top of frozen molecular encoders; speaks directly to the tabular-foundation-model question currently being weighed for property prediction.
- [Li et al., *arXiv*, 2026-07-02](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01800) — **Do LLMs truly generalise in the molecular domain?** Introduces a molecular-perturbation framework to probe whether sequence-based molecular LLMs generalise beyond local token neighbourhoods; a cautionary read before deploying chemical language models.

## Antibiotic and antimicrobial discovery
- [Lepori et al., *Nat Microbiol*, 2026-06-30](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41564-026-02412-5) 🤖 — **Machine learning of chemical features for outer-membrane permeation in mycobacteria.** Profiles mycomembrane permeation of 1,572 azide-tagged compounds in *M. tuberculosis* and *M. smegmatis* via the PAC-MAN click assay, then learns the governing chemical features; a permeation predictor squarely on the E-AMR-CC Gram-negative/mycobacterial accumulation problem. · 🧪
- [Singh et al., *Folia Microbiol*, 2026-07-06](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12223-026-01542-x) 🌍 — **Machine-learning-guided discovery of spirocyclic *M. tuberculosis* FtsZ inhibitors.** India-led computational screen of the Life Chemicals library against the TB cell-division protein FtsZ; adds candidate antitubercular scaffolds to the NTD pipeline space.
- [Lata & Heinz, *bioRxiv*, 2026-07-03](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.03.30.715302) — **ECLIPSE: mapping the dark proteome of ESKAPE pathogens.** Uses sequence-similarity networks to annotate hypothetical proteins across ESKAPE proteomes and surface novel druggable targets; upstream context for AMR target selection.

## Global health and open science
- [Magaña et al., *PLoS Biol*, 2026-07-01](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.3003839) — **Towards globally equitable bioinformatics adoption.** A large multi-continent consortium, with substantial African and Latin American authorship, sets out barriers and priorities for equitable bioinformatics capacity; closely aligned with Ersilia's open-science and LMIC capacity-building mission.
- [Jimale et al., *Infect Drug Resist*, 2026-06-26](https://doi.org/10.2147/idr.s612836) 🌍 — **Antimicrobial resistance in Somalia: surveillance gaps and One Health priorities.** Somali-led narrative review showing sparse, urban-concentrated AMR evidence and weak national surveillance; frames the LMIC data gaps that open AMR tooling aims to help close.
- [Lubwama et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-07-06](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.07.05.736378) 🌍 — **Gram-negative AMR in Ugandan cancer patients with bacteraemia.** Makerere / Uganda Cancer Institute study characterising ESBL-producing Enterobacterales and validating ambient-temperature DNA shipping for low-resource genomic surveillance; LMIC-led AMR work of direct global-health relevance.
- [Viayna et al., *Mol Divers*, 2026-06-28](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11030-026-11640-0) — **Candimine as a scaffold targeting squalene synthetase in *Trypanosoma cruzi*.** Computational target deconvolution of an Amaryllidaceae alkaloid active against *T. cruzi*; adds a natural-product lead and candidate target to Chagas (NTD) drug discovery.
- [Souza et al., *Molecules*, 2026-06-29](https://doi.org/10.3390/molecules31132278) — **Arylidenehydrazinyl thiazole-5-carboxylates as antileishmanial trypanothione-synthetase inhibitors.** Synthesises and evaluates a thiazole series against *Leishmania* with trypanothione synthetase as the proposed target; on-scope NTD antiparasitic medicinal chemistry.
- [Hathaway et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-07-06](https://doi.org/10.64898/2025.12.10.693568) — **The Portable Microhaplotype Object and tools.** Proposes an open, interoperable data standard for targeted-sequencing microhaplotypes (a malaria-surveillance staple); open infrastructure that supports equitable data sharing in global-health genomics.
