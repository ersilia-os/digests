## AI agents and foundation models for science

- [Ding et al., *Nature Machine Intelligence*, 2026-04-29](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-026-01226-8) ⭐🤖 — **Pretraining a foundation model for small-molecule natural products.** First foundation model trained on natural-product chemical space; takes SMILES as input and produces featurizations transferable across NP tasks — drops cleanly into the Hub as a natural-products encoder, a coverage gap the existing generic encoders only partially fill. · 🧩
- [Chen et al., *arXiv*, 2026-05-24](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22287) 🤖 — **SciCore-Mol: augmenting large language models with pluggable molecular cognition modules.** Modular adapter plugging property-prediction, generation, retrosynthesis, and captioning heads onto an LLM backbone, all keyed off SMILES input — a Hub-as-tool-layer reference architecture for any future model-router work. · 🧩
- [Coley, *Daedalus*, 2026](https://www.amacad.org/sites/default/files/publication/downloads/daedalus_wi-sp26_25_coley.pdf) — **AI & the Discovery of Molecules through Autonomous Laboratories.** Coley's "we need new molecules" essay covers medicines for neglected disease, catalysts, agricultural agents — argues for tightly coupling generative chemistry with autonomous experimentation. Pair with the Aspuru-Guzik and Chibale essays in the same special issue.
- [Aspuru-Guzik, *Daedalus*, 2026](https://www.amacad.org/sites/default/files/publication/downloads/daedalus_wi-sp26_04_aspuru-guzik.pdf) — **From Alchemy to AIchemy: On Matter, Minds & Tools.** Essay tracing how information processing reshaped chemistry from alchemy to generative AI — useful framing for positioning the Hub within the broader self-driving-laboratory and AI-for-science ecosystem.
- [Singh, Clerkin et al., *Current Opinion in Structural Biology*, 2026-06](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sbi.2026.103257) — **More protein–ligand data are needed for AlphaFold-like models to enable drug discovery.** Chodera-group perspective on the dataset bottleneck behind cofolding generalisation — directly informs how to scope upcoming protein–ligand benchmarks and which open datasets the Hub roadmap should consume.
- [Hunklinger et al., *Nature Machine Intelligence*, 2026-05-11](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-026-01232-w) ⭐ — **Towards the explainability of protein language models.** Systematic study of what protein LMs encode internally and how to surface it. The model itself takes protein sequences (out of current Hub scope), but the interpretability framework is transferable to chemical encoders.

## AI/ML methods for drug discovery

- [Ruiz-Botella et al., *Nature Machine Intelligence*, 2026-05-04](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-026-01229-5) ⭐🤖 — **A collaborative constrained graph diffusion model for generating realistic synthetic molecules.** Constraint-aware diffusion that outputs small molecules with improved synthetic accessibility — direct candidate for the Hub's generative-chemistry slot, where synthesisability has been a recurring weakness. · 🎨
- [Lee et al., *JCIM*, 2026-05-20](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c00885) 🤖 — **Reliability-aware deep learning framework for chemical genotoxicity prediction with uncertainty quantification.** SMILES-input genotoxicity classifier with calibrated UQ — drop-in candidate alongside existing Hub genotoxicity models, and the UQ component closes a recurring gap in the toxicity track. · 🧪
- [Petrosyan & Zakaryan, *JCIM*, 2026-05-18](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.5c02953) 🤖 — **Machine-learning soft-voting ensemble model for the prediction of oral drug-likeness of chemical structures.** Small-molecule drug-likeness classifier with a transparent ensemble — directly maps onto the Hub's drug-likeness/ADMET tooling. · 🧪
- [He et al., *Bioinformatics*, 2026-05-21](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btag290) 🤖 — **HKD-CPI: high-order knowledge distillation enhanced inductive compound–protein interaction prediction.** Compound-side input is small molecule (SMILES); distils high-order interaction patterns across similar molecules for stronger inductive generalisation to unseen compounds — drop-in alternative to the CPI predictors currently bundled in the Hub. · 🎯
- [D'Anna et al., *ACS Omega*, 2026](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/full/10.1021/acsomega.6c03072) 🤖 — **AGAPE: a machine-learning workflow for G-quadruplex stabilization prediction.** SMILES-input ML model for G4 stabilisation; built on a curated QC-feature dataset — directly Hub-fit and reusable for non-canonical DNA-binding programmes. · 🎯
- [Su et al., *Nucleic Acids Research*, 2026](https://academic.oup.com/nar/article/54/9/gkag488/8687149) 🤖 — **Discovery of a triple-site inhibitor targeting bacterial methionyl-tRNA synthetase through drug repurposing screening and generative AI-assisted optimization.** SMILES-input repurposing screen plus evolution-conservation-aware generative optimisation — *NAR* is a Tier-1 Hub venue and the small-molecule optimisation loop is reproducible. · 🎨
- [Vermeire et al., *ChemRxiv*, 2026-05](https://chemrxiv.org/doi/pdf/10.26434/chemrxiv.15003516) 🗃️ — **QuantumPioneer: scalable generation of quantum chemical data for solution-phase hydrogen-transfer reactions.** Open reaction-centred QM dataset from the Coley/Kraft groups, covering thermochemistry, kinetics, and solvation for radical chemistry — the kind of large, well-published corpus that could anchor a future quantum-property head in Hub featurizers. · 🧪
- [Deng et al., *JCIM*, 2026-05-19](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c00547) — **Comprehensive review of contrastive and generative self-supervised learning for small molecular representation.** Tier-1 review separating contrastive vs generative SSL families for chemistry encoders — exactly the literature an updated Hub featurizer benchmark should index against.
- [Joung et al., *Chem Soc Rev*, 2026](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2026/cs/d6cs00079g) — **An overview of reaction outcome prediction with physics-based and data-driven methods.** Coley-group review tying together QM, template-based, and graph/transformer reaction predictors; useful map of the field for retrosynthesis-aware extensions to the Hub's generative-chemistry stack.
- [Muratspahić et al., *Nature*, 2026-05-21](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10656-8) ⭐ — **De novo design of miniproteins targeting GPCRs.** Computational design plus a "receptor diversion" microscopy screen yields high-affinity, selective miniprotein binders for integral-membrane GPCRs — relevant precedent for partner projects that need ligand alternatives where small-molecule chemistry is exhausted. (Not Hub-fit: output is miniproteins.)
- [Wang et al., *Nature Machine Intelligence*, 2026-04-21](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-026-01223-x) ⭐ — **Predicting RNA 3D structure and conformers using a pre-trained secondary structure model and structure-aware attention.** RNA-input analogue of the AF-family workflow — relevant context for any RNA-targeting small-molecule programme but outside the Hub's current small-molecule-only input surface.
- [Augustine et al., *Nature Machine Intelligence*, 2026-05-18](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-026-01201-3) ⭐ — **Immunotherapy drug target identification using machine learning and patient-derived tumour explant validation.** Multi-omics-input target-ID pipeline benchmarked against ex vivo tumour outcomes — useful template for oncology target prioritisation, but the input modality keeps it out of the small-molecule Hub surface.
- [Deng et al., *JCIM*, 2026-05-18](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c00821) — **ProtSATT: an advanced protein solubility predictor based on attention mechanism.** Attention-based protein-solubility predictor; protein-sequence input keeps it out of the current Hub surface, but worth tracking for downstream pairing with small-molecule formulation models.
- [Masone & Caparotta, *JCIM*, 2026-05-21](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c00424) — **Structural hotspot conditioning for diffusion-based molecular design.** Pocket-conditioned diffusion that outputs small molecules — promising but the pocket-tensor conditioning sits outside the current Hub generator interface, which assumes molecule-only inputs.
- [Ellington et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-13](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.11.724149v1) — **Cell-level virtual screening with CellVS-Net.** SOTA on predicting gene-network restructuring under chemical and genetic perturbations across cell types; primary input is cell context, so not Hub-fit yet, but the perturbational-signature framing aligns with future Hub work on phenotypic screening.
- [Ota, Ito & Shimizu, *bioRxiv*, 2026-04-21](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.04.16.718332v1) — **XACT: dose-dependent modeling of combinatorial drug responses from transcriptomic profiles.** Hierarchical deep-learning framework reconstructing dose-response curves from clinical RNA — context for the repurposing thread; transcriptomic input keeps it out of the Hub small-molecule interface.
- [Katsaouni et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-21](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.20.726471v1) — **RANKOR: direct drug prioritization from bulk and single-cell transcriptomic signatures.** Transcriptomic-input ranker; sits alongside existing signature-based prioritisation pipelines for NTD repurposing but not Hub-incorporable as-is.
- [Allen, Bonnet et al. (Serna Bio), *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-12](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.08.723908v1) — **Generative chemistry platform for small molecules targeting RNA.** Multi-parametric RNA-targeting generator with experimental validation against human medchem; output is small molecules but the RNA-target conditioning is outside the current Hub interface. Worth benchmarking once the Hub supports target-conditioned generators.
- [Dias et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-24](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.20.726737v1) — **Selective scoring of drug effects in multicellular co-culture systems.** Co-culture Efficacy Score (CES) resolves target-effector versus non-specific toxicity in multi-cell screens — useful readout when curating phenotypic-screen datasets for Hub training.
- [Hukerikar et al., *Science Advances*, 2026-05-20](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adz7117) ⭐ — **The immunoproteome and multimorbidity: a Mendelian randomization study.** Cis-MR across 151 immune-proteins and 64 diseases plus a knowledge-graph clustering — the "protein-disease community" output is compatible with target-network views used in systems-pharmacology threads of the Hub.
- [Tang et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-21](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.19.726272v1) — **Reappraisal of GPR40/FFAR1 as a therapeutic target via systematic cheminformatic analysis of 2,637 ChEMBL compounds.** Demonstrates how ChEMBL-scale property-aware re-ranking can salvage a discarded target by separating on-target activity from off-target liability — a reproducible pattern for Ersilia's ADMET-aware re-prioritisation work.

## Antibiotic and antimicrobial discovery

- [Clemens et al., *npj Antimicrobials and Resistance*, 2026-05-22](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44259-026-00218-3) 🗃️ — **COMPASS: a 75,381-sequence AMP database (and AmpGPT2 transformer).** The release of COMPASS — aggregated and deduplicated across nine public AMP repositories — is the headline; it is now the largest open AMP corpus suitable for benchmarking small-molecule mimetic models in the Hub's antimicrobial track. The bundled AmpGPT2 generator is peptide-input and therefore not directly Hub-incorporable. · 🎯
- [Torres et al., *Nature Machine Intelligence*, 2026-05-13](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-026-01237-5) ⭐ — **A generative AI approach for peptide antibiotic optimization.** Peptide-input generator; reframes how to optimise antibiotic peptides under multi-objective constraints. Out of the current Hub small-molecule input surface, but the optimisation loop is reproducible and worth tracking for any future peptide-mimetic work.
- [Kong et al., *JCIM*, 2026-05-18](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c00042) — **Conditional diffusion model–based method for annotation of antibiotic resistance gene properties.** Gene-input generative annotator — complements pathogen-side activity work but does not fit the Hub small-molecule interface.
- [van der Velden et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-22](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.21.726858v1) — **Discovery of a novel chemotype targeting *Mycobacterium tuberculosis* cytochrome bd through rapid screening and structural elucidation.** New TB scaffold against a dual-respiratory-chain target combination — directly relevant to the H3D TB programme and worth tracking for downstream MIC modelling.
- [Rajendran et al., *medRxiv*, 2026-05-22](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.05.20.26353739) — **Integrated machine learning–PanGWAS reveals chromosome-encoded persistence networks and plasmid plasticity in recurrent UTI *E. coli*.** Pan-GWAS plus ML on uropathogenic *E. coli* identifies chromosomal persistence loci distinct from plasmid-borne resistance — useful for designing target/feature sets in upcoming AMR predictive models.
- [Wolff Bengtsen et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-21](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.19.726418v1) — **Pathogen virulence does not alter antibiotic efficacy under growth-matched in vivo conditions.** *Galleria mellonella* infection model decoupling virulence from antibiotic response — sober reminder for any ML model that conflates clinical-isolate virulence with treatability when training on routine MIC datasets.

## Global health and open science

- [Chibale, *Daedalus*, 2026](https://www.amacad.org/sites/default/files/publication/downloads/daedalus_wi-sp26_18_chibale.pdf) — **The Role of AI in Drug Discovery in Africa.** Position essay from the H3D director on how African drug-discovery R&D can absorb AI — directly aligned with the AI2050 and GC-ADDA programmes Ersilia is co-delivering, and worth circulating internally as framing material for partner conversations.
- [Hijri et al., *The ISME Journal*, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1093/ismejo/wrag132) — **Advancing microbial ecology, microbiomes, and One Health in Africa: from regional initiatives to pan-African flagship programs.** ISME-Africa perspective on Africa-specific reference genomes and population-representative microbiome studies — useful context for AMR-relevant microbiome work and for any future Hub coverage of microbiome-derived ML.
- [Nué-Martinez et al., *Communications Chemistry*, 2026-05-22](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42004-026-02073-9) — **Direct amidinium–phosphate interactions guide AT-selective DNA binding by a potent antileishmanial drug.** NMR-resolved mechanism for JNII40, a bis(pyridine-2-carboxamidine) hitting AT-rich kinetoplast DNA — a mechanism class worth flagging for any leishmaniasis prioritisation models that currently treat DNA-binders as a single bucket.
- [Fathallah et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-21](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.15.725345v1) — **Adaptive metabolic reprogramming of the brain during late-stage *Trypanosoma brucei* infection maintains host learning.** Stage-II HAT brain-metabolism study; helpful biological context for any HAT-stage-aware activity model and underlines why CNS penetration remains the discriminating feature for stage-II compounds.
- [Editorial, *Nature Medicine*, 2026-05-21](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-026-04443-1) ⭐ — **Combating infectious diseases in a fragmented world.** Top-of-issue editorial framing infectious-disease research priorities against funding fragmentation; worth a careful read given parallel *Lancet Microbe* data on declining antibacterial R&D investment.
- [Zuckerman & Juavinett, *PLOS Comput Biol*, 2026-02-23](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1013970) — **Ten simple rules for building a collaborative coding culture.** Evidence-based recommendations distilled from 25+ researcher interviews on growing computational practice inside non-computational labs — directly relevant to Ersilia's capacity-building partnerships in Africa and to onboarding new Hub contributors.
- [Emma, *ChEMBL blog*, 2026-05-19](https://chembl.blogspot.com/2026/05/the-first-protac-has-been-approved-can.html) — **The first PROTAC has been approved: can we find it in ChEMBL?** ChEMBL is curating degrader–target interactions and introducing a MODALITY flag in v37 — relevant infrastructure for the `glueAI` molecular-glue track and for any future Hub model on targeted protein degradation.
