## AI agents and foundation models for science

- [Ding et al., *Nature Machine Intelligence*, 2026-04-29](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-026-01226-8) ⭐🤖 — **Pretraining a foundation model for small-molecule natural products.** First foundation model trained on natural-product chemical space — directly fills a gap the Hub currently covers with generic encoders, and natural-product downstream tasks dominate African DDI partner portfolios. · 🧩
- [Hunklinger et al., *Nature Machine Intelligence*, 2026-05-11](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-026-01232-w) ⭐ — **Towards the explainability of protein language models.** Systematic study of what protein LMs encode internally and how to surface it — relevant template for adding interpretability layers to Hub protein-side encoders.
- [Coley, *Daedalus*, 2026](https://www.amacad.org/sites/default/files/publication/downloads/daedalus_wi-sp26_25_coley.pdf) — **AI & the Discovery of Molecules through Autonomous Laboratories.** Coley's "we need new molecules" essay covers medicines for neglected disease, catalysts, agricultural agents — argues for tightly coupling generative chemistry with autonomous experimentation. Pair with the Aspuru-Guzik and Chibale essays in the same special issue.
- [Aspuru-Guzik, *Daedalus*, 2026](https://www.amacad.org/sites/default/files/publication/downloads/daedalus_wi-sp26_04_aspuru-guzik.pdf) — **From Alchemy to AIchemy: On Matter, Minds & Tools.** Essay tracing how information processing reshaped chemistry from alchemy to generative AI — useful framing for positioning the Hub within the broader self-driving-laboratory and AI-for-science ecosystem.
- [Singh, Clerkin et al., *Current Opinion in Structural Biology*, 2026-06](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sbi.2026.103257) — **More protein–ligand data are needed for AlphaFold-like models to enable drug discovery.** Chodera-group perspective on the dataset bottleneck behind cofolding generalisation — directly informs how to scope upcoming protein–ligand benchmarks in the Hub roadmap.
- [Chen et al., *arXiv*, 2026-05-24](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22287) 🤖 — **SciCore-Mol: augmenting large language models with pluggable molecular cognition modules.** Modular adapter plugging property-prediction, generation, retrosynthesis, and captioning heads onto an LLM backbone — a Hub-as-tool-layer reference architecture for any future model-router work. · 🧩

## AI/ML methods for drug discovery

- [Muratspahić et al., *Nature*, 2026-05-21](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10656-8) ⭐ — **De novo design of miniproteins targeting GPCRs.** Computational design plus a "receptor diversion" microscopy screen yields high-affinity, selective miniprotein binders for integral-membrane GPCRs — relevant precedent for partner projects that need ligand alternatives where small-molecule chemistry is exhausted.
- [Ruiz-Botella et al., *Nature Machine Intelligence*, 2026-05-04](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-026-01229-5) ⭐🤖 — **A collaborative constrained graph diffusion model for generating realistic synthetic molecules.** Constraint-aware diffusion that improves synthetic accessibility versus standard generators — direct candidate for the Hub's generative-chemistry track where downstream synthesisability has been a recurring bottleneck. · 🎨
- [Wang et al., *Nature Machine Intelligence*, 2026-04-21](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-026-01223-x) ⭐🤖 — **Predicting RNA 3D structure and conformers using a pre-trained secondary structure model and structure-aware attention.** RNA-targeted analogue of the AF-family workflow — useful candidate for any RNA-side pipeline and complementary to ongoing RNA-binder generative work. · 🗺️
- [Augustine et al., *Nature Machine Intelligence*, 2026-05-18](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-026-01201-3) ⭐🤖 — **Immunotherapy drug target identification using machine learning and patient-derived tumour explant validation.** Target-ID pipeline benchmarked against ex vivo tumour outcomes — replicable template for Hub target-prioritisation models on oncology endpoints. · 🎯
- [Lee et al., *JCIM*, 2026-05-20](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c00885) 🤖 — **Reliability-aware deep learning framework for chemical genotoxicity prediction with uncertainty quantification.** Genotoxicity model with calibrated UQ — drop-in candidate alongside existing Hub genotoxicity models, and the UQ component closes a recurring gap in the toxicity track. · 🧪
- [Petrosyan & Zakaryan, *JCIM*, 2026-05-18](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.5c02953) 🤖 — **Machine-learning soft-voting ensemble model for the prediction of oral drug-likeness of chemical structures.** Drug-likeness classifier with a transparent ensemble — directly maps onto the Hub's drug-likeness/ADMET tooling. · 🧪
- [Deng et al., *JCIM*, 2026-05-18](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c00821) 🤖 — **ProtSATT: an advanced protein solubility predictor based on attention mechanism.** Attention-based protein-solubility predictor — a complement to the small-molecule solubility models already in the Hub and an entry point for protein-property coverage. · 🧪
- [Masone & Caparotta, *JCIM*, 2026-05-21](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c00424) 🤖 — **Structural hotspot conditioning for diffusion-based molecular design.** Pocket-hotspot-guided diffusion — a more constrained alternative to DiffSBDD-style generators for structure-aware design pipelines. · 🎨
- [Deng et al., *JCIM*, 2026-05-19](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c00547) — **Comprehensive review of contrastive and generative self-supervised learning for small molecular representation.** Tier-1 review separating contrastive vs generative SSL families for chemistry encoders — exactly the literature an updated Hub featurizer benchmark should index against.
- [Joung et al., *Chem Soc Rev*, 2026](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2026/cs/d6cs00079g) — **An overview of reaction outcome prediction with physics-based and data-driven methods.** Coley-group review tying together QM, template-based, and graph/transformer reaction predictors; useful map of the field for retrosynthesis-aware extensions to the Hub's generative-chemistry stack.
- [He et al., *Bioinformatics*, 2026-05-21](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btag290) 🤖 — **HKD-CPI: high-order knowledge distillation enhanced inductive compound–protein interaction prediction.** Distils high-order interaction patterns across similar molecules for stronger inductive generalisation — drop-in alternative to the CPI predictors currently bundled in the Hub. · 🎯
- [Ellington et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-13](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.11.724149v1) 🤖 — **Cell-level virtual screening with CellVS-Net.** SOTA on predicting gene-network restructuring under chemical and genetic perturbations across cell types — directly Hub-aligned for the perturbational-signature line of work. · 🎯
- [Ota, Ito & Shimizu, *bioRxiv*, 2026-04-21](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.04.16.718332v1) 🤖 — **XACT: dose-dependent modeling of combinatorial drug responses from transcriptomic profiles.** Hierarchical deep-learning framework reconstructing full dose-response curves from clinically accessible RNA — strong fit for the Hub's repurposing / transcriptomic-prioritisation thread. · 🎯
- [Vermeire et al., *ChemRxiv*, 2026-05](https://chemrxiv.org/doi/pdf/10.26434/chemrxiv.15003516) 🗃️ — **QuantumPioneer: scalable generation of quantum chemical data for solution-phase hydrogen-transfer reactions.** Open reaction-centred QM dataset (Coley/Kraft groups) covering thermochemistry, kinetics, and solvation for radical chemistry — primary candidate corpus for any quantum-property head added to Hub featurizers. · 🧪
- [Katsaouni et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-21](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.20.726471v1) 🤖 — **RANKOR: direct drug prioritization from bulk and single-cell transcriptomic signatures.** Drug ranking straight from disease signatures without GSEA-style enrichment, on bulk and single-cell inputs — sits alongside existing Hub signature-based prioritisation pipelines for NTD repurposing. · 🎯
- [D'Anna et al., *ACS Omega*, 2026](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/full/10.1021/acsomega.6c03072) 🤖 — **AGAPE: a machine-learning workflow for G-quadruplex stabilization prediction.** First ML model for G4 stabilisation built on a curated QC-feature dataset — reusable for any GPCR/nucleic-acid-target Hub model that touches non-canonical DNA structures. · 🎯
- [Allen, Bonnet et al. (Serna Bio), *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-12](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.08.723908v1) 🤖 — **Generative chemistry platform for small molecules targeting RNA.** Multi-parametric RNA-targeting generator with experimental validation against human medchem — relevant to growing RNA-binder interest and benchmarkable against the Hub's small-molecule generators. · 🎨
- [Dias et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-24](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.20.726737v1) — **Selective scoring of drug effects in multicellular co-culture systems.** Co-culture Efficacy Score (CES) resolves target-effector versus non-specific toxicity in multi-cell screens — useful readout when curating phenotypic-screen datasets for Hub training.
- [Hukerikar et al., *Science Advances*, 2026-05-20](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adz7117) ⭐ — **The immunoproteome and multimorbidity: a Mendelian randomization study.** Cis-MR across 151 immune-proteins and 64 diseases plus a knowledge-graph clustering — the "protein-disease community" output is compatible with target-network views used in systems-pharmacology threads of the Hub.
- [Tang et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-21](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.19.726272v1) — **Reappraisal of GPR40/FFAR1 as a therapeutic target via systematic cheminformatic analysis of 2,637 ChEMBL compounds.** Demonstrates how ChEMBL-scale property-aware re-ranking can salvage a discarded target by separating on-target activity from off-target liability — a reproducible pattern for Ersilia's ADMET-aware re-prioritisation work.

## Antibiotic and antimicrobial discovery

- [Torres et al., *Nature Machine Intelligence*, 2026-05-13](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-026-01237-5) ⭐🤖 — **A generative AI approach for peptide antibiotic optimization.** Direct Hub-relevant generator for antibiotic peptides — pairs naturally with the COMPASS / AmpGPT2 release in *npj AMR* this week and fits the Grand Challenges Gram-negative AMR brief. · 🎨
- [Clemens et al., *npj Antimicrobials and Resistance*, 2026-05-22](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44259-026-00218-3) 🤖🗃️ — **Harnessing generative AI for predicting and optimizing antimicrobial peptides against drug-resistant infections.** Releases COMPASS, a 75k-sequence AMP database, and AmpGPT2, a transformer fine-tuned for AMP generation — both clean candidates for the Hub's antibiotic-peptide track. · 🎨
- [Su et al., *Nucleic Acids Research*, 2026](https://academic.oup.com/nar/article/54/9/gkag488/8687149) 🤖 — **Discovery of a triple-site inhibitor targeting bacterial methionyl-tRNA synthetase through drug repurposing screening and generative AI-assisted optimization.** Repurposing screen plus evolution-conservation-aware generative optimisation against MetRS — *NAR* is a Tier-1 Hub venue and the optimisation loop is reproducible. · 🎨
- [Kong et al., *JCIM*, 2026-05-18](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c00042) 🤖 — **Conditional diffusion model–based method for annotation of antibiotic resistance gene properties.** Generative annotator over AMR gene catalogues — complements the Hub's pathogen-side activity models with a gene-level resistance prior. · 🎯
- [van der Velden et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-22](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.21.726858v1) — **Discovery of a novel chemotype targeting *Mycobacterium tuberculosis* cytochrome bd through rapid screening and structural elucidation.** New TB scaffold against a dual-respiratory-chain target combination — directly relevant to the H3D TB programme and worth tracking for downstream MIC modelling.
- [Rajendran et al., *medRxiv*, 2026-05-22](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.05.20.26353739) — **Integrated machine learning–PanGWAS reveals chromosome-encoded persistence networks and plasmid plasticity in recurrent UTI *E. coli*.** Pan-GWAS plus ML on uropathogenic *E. coli* identifies chromosomal persistence loci distinct from plasmid-borne resistance — useful for designing target/feature sets in upcoming AMR predictive models.
- [Wolff Bengtsen et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-21](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.19.726418v1) — **Pathogen virulence does not alter antibiotic efficacy under growth-matched in vivo conditions.** *Galleria mellonella* infection model decoupling virulence from antibiotic response — sober reminder for any ML model that conflates clinical-isolate virulence with treatability when training on routine MIC datasets.

## Global health and open science

- [Chibale, *Daedalus*, 2026](https://www.amacad.org/sites/default/files/publication/downloads/daedalus_wi-sp26_18_chibale.pdf) — **The Role of AI in Drug Discovery in Africa.** Position essay from the H3D director on how African drug-discovery R&D can absorb AI — directly aligned with the AI2050 and GC-ADDA programmes Ersilia is co-delivering, and worth circulating internally as framing material for partner conversations.
- [Hijri et al., *The ISME Journal*, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1093/ismejo/wrag132) — **Advancing microbial ecology, microbiomes, and One Health in Africa: from regional initiatives to pan-African flagship programs.** ISME-Africa perspective on Africa-specific reference genomes and population-representative microbiome studies — useful context for AMR-relevant microbiome work and for any future Hub coverage of microbiome-derived ML.
- [Nué-Martinez et al., *Communications Chemistry*, 2026-05-22](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42004-026-02073-9) — **Direct amidinium–phosphate interactions guide AT-selective DNA binding by a potent antileishmanial drug.** NMR-resolved mechanism for JNII40, a bis(pyridine-2-carboxamidine) hitting AT-rich kinetoplast DNA — a mechanism class worth flagging for any leishmaniasis prioritisation models that currently treat DNA-binders as a single bucket.
- [Fathallah et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-21](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.15.725345v1) — **Adaptive metabolic reprogramming of the brain during late-stage *Trypanosoma brucei* infection maintains host learning.** Stage-II HAT brain-metabolism study; helpful biological context for any HAT-stage-aware activity model and underlines why CNS penetration remains the discriminating feature for stage-II compounds.
- [Editorial, *Nature Medicine*, 2026-05-21](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-026-04443-1) ⭐ — **Combating infectious diseases in a fragmented world.** Top-of-issue editorial framing infectious-disease research priorities against funding fragmentation; worth a careful read given parallel *Lancet Microbe* data on declining antibacterial R&D investment.
- [Zuckerman & Juavinett, *PLOS Comput Biol*, 2026-02-23](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1013970) — **Ten simple rules for building a collaborative coding culture.** Evidence-based recommendations distilled from 25+ researcher interviews on growing computational practice inside non-computational labs — directly relevant to Ersilia's capacity-building partnerships in Africa and to onboarding new Hub contributors.
- [Emma, *ChEMBL blog*, 2026-05-19](https://chembl.blogspot.com/2026/05/the-first-protac-has-been-approved-can.html) — **The first PROTAC has been approved: can we find it in ChEMBL?** ChEMBL is curating degrader–target interactions and introducing a MODALITY flag in v37 — relevant infrastructure for the `glueAI` molecular-glue track and for any future Hub model on targeted protein degradation.
