## AI agents and foundation models for science

- [Ding et al., *Nature Machine Intelligence*, 2026-04-29](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-026-01226-8) ⭐🤖 — **Pretraining a foundation model for small-molecule natural products.** First foundation model trained on natural-product chemical space; takes SMILES as input and produces featurizations transferable across NP tasks — drops cleanly into the Hub as a natural-products encoder, a coverage gap the existing generic encoders only partially fill. · 🧩
- [Chen et al., *arXiv*, 2026-05-24](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22287) 🤖 — **SciCore-Mol: augmenting large language models with pluggable molecular cognition modules.** Modular adapter plugging property-prediction, generation, retrosynthesis, and captioning heads onto an LLM backbone, all keyed off SMILES input — a Hub-as-tool-layer reference architecture for any future model-router work. · 🧩
- [Coley, *Daedalus*, 2026](https://www.amacad.org/sites/default/files/publication/downloads/daedalus_wi-sp26_25_coley.pdf) — **AI & the Discovery of Molecules through Autonomous Laboratories.** Argues for tightly coupling generative chemistry with autonomous experimentation, covering medicines for neglected disease and pandemic preparedness; pair with the Chibale Africa-DDI essay in the same special issue.
- [Singh, Clerkin et al., *Current Opinion in Structural Biology*, 2026-06](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sbi.2026.103257) — **More protein–ligand data are needed for AlphaFold-like models to enable drug discovery.** Chodera-group perspective on the dataset bottleneck behind cofolding generalisation — directly informs how to scope upcoming protein–ligand benchmarks and which open datasets the Hub roadmap should consume.

## AI/ML methods for drug discovery

- [Ruiz-Botella et al., *Nature Machine Intelligence*, 2026-05-04](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-026-01229-5) ⭐🤖 — **A collaborative constrained graph diffusion model for generating realistic synthetic molecules.** Constraint-aware diffusion that outputs small molecules with improved synthetic accessibility — direct candidate for the Hub's generative-chemistry slot, where synthesisability has been a recurring weakness. · 🎨
- [Lee et al., *JCIM*, 2026-05-20](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c00885) 🤖 — **Reliability-aware deep learning framework for chemical genotoxicity prediction with uncertainty quantification.** SMILES-input genotoxicity classifier with calibrated UQ — drop-in candidate alongside existing Hub genotoxicity models, and the UQ component closes a recurring gap in the toxicity track. · 🧪
- [Petrosyan & Zakaryan, *JCIM*, 2026-05-18](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.5c02953) 🤖 — **Machine-learning soft-voting ensemble model for the prediction of oral drug-likeness of chemical structures.** Small-molecule drug-likeness classifier with a transparent ensemble — directly maps onto the Hub's drug-likeness/ADMET tooling. · 🧪
- [He et al., *Bioinformatics*, 2026-05-21](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btag290) 🤖 — **HKD-CPI: high-order knowledge distillation enhanced inductive compound–protein interaction prediction.** SMILES-input model distilling high-order interaction patterns across similar molecules for stronger inductive generalisation to unseen compounds — drop-in alternative to the CPI predictors currently bundled in the Hub. · 🎯
- [Deng et al., *JCIM*, 2026-05-19](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c00547) — **Comprehensive review of contrastive and generative self-supervised learning for small molecular representation.** Tier-1 review separating contrastive vs generative SSL families for chemistry encoders — exactly the literature an updated Hub featurizer benchmark should index against.
- [Westerlund et al., *JCIM*, 2026-05-23](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c01147) — **Toward lab-ready AI synthesis plans with protection strategies and route scoring.** Synthesis-planning model that scores AI-generated routes against lab-readiness criteria and inserts protecting-group strategies automatically — a credible front-end to any Hub generative model whose downstream bottleneck is synthesisability.
- [Baumann et al., *JCIM*, 2026-05-21](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c00089) — **Large-scale collaborative assessment of binding free energy calculations for drug discovery using OpenFE.** Open-source FEP benchmark coordinated across multiple labs — sets the current state-of-the-art baseline for protein–ligand affinity prediction that any Hub-side activity model now has to be measured against.
- [Joung et al., *Chem Soc Rev*, 2026](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2026/cs/d6cs00079g) — **An overview of reaction outcome prediction with physics-based and data-driven methods.** Coley-group review tying together QM, template-based, and graph/transformer reaction predictors; useful map of the field for retrosynthesis-aware extensions to the Hub's generative-chemistry stack.

## Antibiotic and antimicrobial discovery

- [Su et al., *Nucleic Acids Research*, 2026](https://academic.oup.com/nar/article/54/9/gkag488/8687149) 🤖 — **Discovery of a triple-site inhibitor targeting bacterial methionyl-tRNA synthetase through drug repurposing screening and generative AI-assisted optimization.** SMILES-input repurposing screen plus evolution-conservation-aware generative optimisation against MetRS — *NAR* is a Tier-1 Hub venue and the small-molecule optimisation loop is directly reusable for the Hub antibacterial track. · 🎨
- [Clemens et al., *npj Antimicrobials and Resistance*, 2026-05-22](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44259-026-00218-3) 🗃️ — **COMPASS: a 75,381-sequence AMP database (and AmpGPT2 transformer).** The release of COMPASS — aggregated and deduplicated across nine public AMP repositories — is the headline; it is now the largest open AMP corpus suitable for benchmarking small-molecule mimetic models in the Hub's antimicrobial track. The bundled AmpGPT2 generator is peptide-input and therefore not directly Hub-incorporable. · 🎯
- [Torres et al., *Nature Machine Intelligence*, 2026-05-13](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-026-01237-5) ⭐ — **A generative AI approach for peptide antibiotic optimization.** Peptide-input multi-objective optimiser for antibiotic peptides; out of the current Hub small-molecule input surface but the optimisation loop is reproducible and worth tracking for any future peptide-mimetic work.
- [Kong et al., *JCIM*, 2026-05-18](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c00042) — **Conditional diffusion model–based method for annotation of antibiotic resistance gene properties.** Gene-input generative annotator over AMR gene catalogues — complements pathogen-side activity work; does not fit the Hub small-molecule interface but the curated AMR-gene labels are reusable.
- [van der Velden et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-22](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.21.726858v1) — **Discovery of a novel chemotype targeting *Mycobacterium tuberculosis* cytochrome bd through rapid screening and structural elucidation.** New TB scaffold against a dual-respiratory-chain target combination — directly relevant to the H3D TB programme and worth tracking for downstream MIC modelling.
- [Rajendran et al., *medRxiv*, 2026-05-22](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.05.20.26353739) — **Integrated machine learning–PanGWAS reveals chromosome-encoded persistence networks and plasmid plasticity in recurrent UTI *E. coli*.** Pan-GWAS plus ML on uropathogenic *E. coli* identifies chromosomal persistence loci distinct from plasmid-borne resistance — useful for designing target/feature sets in upcoming AMR predictive models.
- [Wolff Bengtsen et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-21](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.19.726418v1) — **Pathogen virulence does not alter antibiotic efficacy under growth-matched in vivo conditions.** *Galleria mellonella* infection model decoupling virulence from antibiotic response — sober reminder for any ML model that conflates clinical-isolate virulence with treatability when training on routine MIC datasets.
- [Dave et al., *Archives of Microbiology*, 2026-05-25](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00203-026-04979-3) 🌍 — **Overcoming LPS-mediated resistance in gram-negative pathogens: a review of LL-37 analogs and computational design strategies.** Indian-led review covering computational design of LL-37 antimicrobial-peptide analogs against Gram-negative LPS-mediated resistance — useful map of the AMP-design landscape that the Hub's antimicrobial-peptide track should pull from when scoping mimetic small molecules.
- [Midha et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-22](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.21.727054v1) 🌍 — **Genomic characterization and therapeutic potential of five broad-spectrum lytic bacteriophages against multidrug-resistant avian-pathogenic *E. coli*.** Indian-led phage-therapy lead set against MDR APEC isolates with One-Health spillover risk — adjacent to Ersilia's interest in non-small-molecule modalities for resistant Gram-negatives.
- [Duan et al., *Expert Opinion on Drug Discovery*, 2026-05-19](https://doi.org/10.1080/17460441.2026.2674968) — **Artificial intelligence in antimicrobial drug discovery: predictive and generative strategies.** Compact review of how predictive and generative pipelines are being combined for antimicrobials — handy short reference for onboarding new contributors to the Hub's antibiotic models.

## Global health and open science

- [Chibale, *Daedalus*, 2026](https://www.amacad.org/sites/default/files/publication/downloads/daedalus_wi-sp26_18_chibale.pdf) — **The Role of AI in Drug Discovery in Africa.** Position essay from the H3D director on how African drug-discovery R&D can absorb AI — directly aligned with the AI2050 and GC-ADDA programmes Ersilia is co-delivering, and worth circulating internally as framing material for partner conversations.
- [Hijri et al., *The ISME Journal*, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1093/ismejo/wrag132) — **Advancing microbial ecology, microbiomes, and One Health in Africa: from regional initiatives to pan-African flagship programs.** ISME-Africa perspective on Africa-specific reference genomes and population-representative microbiome studies — useful context for AMR-relevant microbiome work and for any future Hub coverage of microbiome-derived ML.
- [Nué-Martinez et al., *Communications Chemistry*, 2026-05-22](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42004-026-02073-9) — **Direct amidinium–phosphate interactions guide AT-selective DNA binding by a potent antileishmanial drug.** NMR-resolved mechanism for JNII40, a bis(pyridine-2-carboxamidine) hitting AT-rich kinetoplast DNA — a mechanism class worth flagging for any leishmaniasis prioritisation models that currently treat DNA-binders as a single bucket.
- [Fathallah et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-21](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.15.725345v1) — **Adaptive metabolic reprogramming of the brain during late-stage *Trypanosoma brucei* infection maintains host learning.** Stage-II HAT brain-metabolism study; helpful biological context for any HAT-stage-aware activity model and underlines why CNS penetration remains the discriminating feature for stage-II compounds.
- [Editorial, *Nature Medicine*, 2026-05-21](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-026-04443-1) ⭐ — **Combating infectious diseases in a fragmented world.** Top-of-issue editorial framing infectious-disease research priorities against funding fragmentation; worth a careful read given parallel *Lancet Microbe* data on declining antibacterial R&D investment.
- [Zuckerman & Juavinett, *PLOS Comput Biol*, 2026-02-23](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1013970) — **Ten simple rules for building a collaborative coding culture.** Evidence-based recommendations distilled from 25+ researcher interviews on growing computational practice inside non-computational labs — directly relevant to Ersilia's capacity-building partnerships in Africa and to onboarding new Hub contributors.
- [Emma, *ChEMBL blog*, 2026-05-19](https://chembl.blogspot.com/2026/05/the-first-protac-has-been-approved-can.html) — **The first PROTAC has been approved: can we find it in ChEMBL?** ChEMBL is curating degrader–target interactions and introducing a MODALITY flag in v37 — relevant infrastructure for the `glueAI` molecular-glue track and for any future Hub model on targeted protein degradation.
