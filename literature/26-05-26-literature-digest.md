## AI agents and foundation models for science

A *Daedalus* "AI & Science" special issue lands this week with three essays the team should triage together — they form a coherent answer to "what should an open AI-for-chemistry organisation be doing in 2026?":

- [Aspuru-Guzik, *Daedalus*, 2026](https://www.amacad.org/sites/default/files/publication/downloads/daedalus_wi-sp26_04_aspuru-guzik.pdf) — **From Alchemy to AIchemy: On Matter, Minds & Tools.** Essay tracing how information processing reshaped chemistry from alchemy to generative AI — useful framing for positioning the Ersilia Model Hub within the broader self-driving-laboratory and AI-for-science ecosystem.
- [Coley, *Daedalus*, 2026](https://www.amacad.org/sites/default/files/publication/downloads/daedalus_wi-sp26_25_coley.pdf) — **AI & the Discovery of Molecules through Autonomous Laboratories.** Coley's "we need new molecules" essay covers medicines for neglected disease, catalysts, agricultural agents — argues for tightly coupling generative chemistry with autonomous experimentation. Read alongside the Aspuru-Guzik and Chibale essays in the same issue.
- [Chibale, *Daedalus*, 2026](https://www.amacad.org/sites/default/files/publication/downloads/daedalus_wi-sp26_18_chibale.pdf) — **The Role of AI in Drug Discovery in Africa.** Position essay from the H3D director on how African drug-discovery R&D can absorb AI — directly aligned with the AI2050 and GC-ADDA programmes Ersilia is co-delivering, and worth circulating internally as framing material for partner conversations.
- [Xie et al., *Nature Biomedical Engineering*, 2026-05](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41551-026-01676-w) ⭐🤖 — **Bridging the interpretability gap for medical AI models using class-association manifold learning.** Generative manifold method that decouples diagnostic class patterns from individual-level background, producing low-dimensional topology maps and contrastive examples while preserving accuracy; the framework is transferable to QSAR explainability and would complement uncertainty layers already explored across Hub models. · 🗺️
- [Chen et al., *arXiv*, 2026-05-24](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22287) — **SciCore-Mol: augmenting large language models with pluggable molecular cognition modules.** Modular adapter design that plugs property-prediction, generation, retrosynthesis, and captioning heads onto an LLM backbone — relevant precedent for a Hub-as-tool-layer behind an LLM agent.

## AI/ML methods for drug discovery

- [Muratspahić et al., *Nature*, 2026-05-21](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10656-8) ⭐ — **De novo design of miniproteins targeting GPCRs.** Computational design plus a "receptor diversion" microscopy screen yields high-affinity, selective miniprotein binders for integral-membrane GPCRs — relevant precedent for any Ersilia partner project that needs ligand alternatives where small-molecule chemistry is exhausted.
- [Joung et al., *Chem Soc Rev*, 2026](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2026/cs/d6cs00079g) — **An overview of reaction outcome prediction with physics-based and data-driven methods.** Coley-group review tying together QM, template-based, and graph/transformer reaction predictors; useful map of the field for any retrosynthesis-aware extensions to the Hub's generative-chemistry stack.
- [Deng et al., *JCIM*, 2026](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/abs/10.1021/acs.jcim.6c00547) — **Comprehensive review of contrastive and generative self-supervised learning for small molecular representation.** Tier-1 review separating contrastive vs generative SSL families for chemistry encoders, with single- vs multi-view analyses — exactly the literature an updated Hub featurizer benchmark should index against.
- [Vermeire et al., *ChemRxiv*, 2026-05](https://chemrxiv.org/doi/pdf/10.26434/chemrxiv.15003516) 🗃️ — **QuantumPioneer: scalable generation of quantum chemical data for solution-phase hydrogen-transfer reactions.** Open reaction-centred QM dataset (with the Coley/Kraft groups) covering thermochemistry, kinetics, and solvation for radical chemistry — a primary candidate corpus for any quantum-property head added to Hub featurizers. · 🧪
- [He et al., *Bioinformatics*, 2026-05-21](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btag290) 🤖 — **HKD-CPI: high-order knowledge distillation enhanced inductive compound–protein interaction prediction.** New CPI model that explicitly distils high-order interaction patterns across similar molecules, with stronger inductive generalisation to unseen compounds — a credible drop-in alternative to the CPI predictors currently bundled in the Hub. · 🎯
- [D'Anna et al., *ACS Omega*, 2026](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/full/10.1021/acsomega.6c03072) 🤖 — **AGAPE: a machine-learning workflow for G-quadruplex stabilization prediction.** First ML model for G4 stabilisation built on a curated QC-feature dataset; the data shape and DFT-derived descriptors are reusable for any GPCR/nucleic-acid-target Hub model that touches non-canonical DNA structures. · 🎯
- [Katsaouni et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-21](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.20.726471v1) 🤖 — **RANKOR: direct drug prioritization from bulk and single-cell transcriptomic signatures.** ML framework that ranks candidate drugs straight from disease signatures without GSEA-style enrichment, working on both bulk and single-cell inputs; sits naturally alongside signature-based prioritisation pipelines the Hub already supports for NTD repurposing. · 🎯
- [Dias et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-24](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.20.726737v1) — **Selective scoring of drug effects in multicellular co-culture systems.** Co-culture Efficacy Score (CES) resolves target-effector versus non-specific toxicity in multi-cell screens — a useful readout to consider when curating phenotypic-screen datasets for Hub training, where the standard dose-response collapse hides mechanism.
- [Hukerikar et al., *Science Advances*, 2026-05-20](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adz7117) ⭐ — **The immunoproteome and multimorbidity: a Mendelian randomization study.** Cis-MR across 151 immune-proteins and 64 diseases plus a knowledge-graph clustering — the "protein-disease community" output is directly compatible with target-network views used in systems-pharmacology threads of the Hub.
- [Tang et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-21](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.19.726272v1) — **Reappraisal of GPR40/FFAR1 as a therapeutic target via systematic cheminformatic analysis of 2,637 ChEMBL compounds.** Demonstrates how ChEMBL-scale property-aware re-ranking can salvage a discarded target by separating on-target activity from off-target liability — a reproducible pattern for Ersilia's ADMET-aware re-prioritisation work.
- [Zhang et al., *PLOS Comput Biol*, 2026-05-22](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1014315) — **Integrated computational and experimental analysis explores FOLH1/PSMA expression patterns across cancers.** Pan-cancer FOLH1 atlas combining transcriptomics, ML classification and validation; useful template for the Hub's pharmacogenomics-adjacent models that bridge tissue-of-origin signals with druggability.

## Antibiotic and antimicrobial discovery

- [Clemens et al., *npj Antimicrobials and Resistance*, 2026-05-22](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44259-026-00218-3) 🤖🗃️ — **Harnessing generative AI for predicting and optimizing antimicrobial peptides against drug-resistant infections.** Releases COMPASS, a 75k-sequence AMP database, and AmpGPT2, a transformer fine-tuned for AMP generation — both are clean candidates for the Hub's antibiotic-peptide track and directly relevant to Grand Challenges Gram-negative AMR work. · 🎨
- [Su et al., *Nucleic Acids Research*, 2026](https://academic.oup.com/nar/article/54/9/gkag488/8687149) 🤖 — **Discovery of a triple-site inhibitor targeting bacterial methionyl-tRNA synthetase through drug repurposing screening and generative AI-assisted optimization.** Repurposing screen plus evolution-conservation-aware generative optimisation against MetRS, a validated *Trypanosoma* and Gram-positive bacterial target — *NAR* is a Tier-1 Hub venue and the optimisation loop is reproducible. · 🎨
- [Midha et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-22](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.21.727054v1) — **Genomic characterization and therapeutic potential of five broad-spectrum lytic bacteriophages against multidrug-resistant avian-pathogenic E. coli.** Phage-therapy lead set against MDR APEC isolates with One-Health spillover risk — adjacent to Ersilia's interest in non-small-molecule modalities for resistant Gram-negatives.
- [Rajendran et al., *medRxiv*, 2026-05-22](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.05.20.26353739) — **Integrated machine learning–PanGWAS reveals chromosome-encoded persistence networks and plasmid plasticity in recurrent UTI E. coli.** Pan-GWAS plus ML on uropathogenic *E. coli* isolates identifies chromosomal persistence loci distinct from plasmid-borne resistance — useful for designing target/feature sets in upcoming AMR predictive models.
- [Wolff Bengtsen et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-21](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.19.726418v1) — **Pathogen virulence does not alter antibiotic efficacy under growth-matched in vivo conditions.** *Galleria mellonella* infection model decoupling virulence from antibiotic response — a sober reminder for any ML model that conflates clinical-isolate virulence with treatability when training on routine MIC datasets.
- [Bookout et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-23](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.22.727335v1) — **Narcosis biosensor for the detection of bacterial membrane disruption by naphthenic acids.** Whole-cell biosensor that distinguishes narcosis from specific membrane disruption; the data shape is appealing for training liability classifiers that flag membrane-active false positives in antibiotic screens.
- [Kamilari et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-21](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.21.726617v1) — **Putative Herbicolin A, an antifungal lipopeptide from *Pantoea agglomerans* APC 4211.** Broad-spectrum natural-product lead with biocontrol potential against food-spoilage moulds; adjacent to the natural-product / antifungal threads of GC-ADDA partner work.
- [Duan et al., *Expert Opinion on Drug Discovery*, 2026](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/17460441.2026.2674968) — **Artificial intelligence in antimicrobial drug discovery: predictive and generative strategies.** Compact review of how predictive and generative pipelines are being combined for antimicrobials — handy short reference for onboarding new contributors to the Hub's antibiotic models.

## Global health and open science

- [Nué-Martinez et al., *Communications Chemistry*, 2026-05-22](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42004-026-02073-9) — **Direct amidinium–phosphate interactions guide AT-selective DNA binding by a potent antileishmanial drug.** NMR-resolved mechanism for JNII40, a bis(pyridine-2-carboxamidine) hitting AT-rich kinetoplast DNA — a mechanism class worth flagging for any leishmaniasis prioritisation models that currently treat DNA-binders as a single bucket.
- [Fathallah et al., *bioRxiv*, 2026-05-21](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.15.725345v1) — **Adaptive metabolic reprogramming of the brain during late-stage *Trypanosoma brucei* infection maintains host learning.** Stage-II HAT brain-metabolism study; helpful biological context for any HAT-stage-aware activity model and underlines why CNS penetration remains the discriminating feature for stage-II compounds.
- [Editorial, *Nature Medicine*, 2026-05-21](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-026-04443-1) ⭐ — **Combating infectious diseases in a fragmented world.** Top-of-issue editorial framing infectious-disease research priorities against funding fragmentation; worth a careful read given parallel *Lancet Microbe* data on declining antibacterial R&D investment.
- [*Nature Briefing*, 2026-05-22](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-01622-x) — **Ebola outbreak: alarming trajectory and possible origins.** Briefing on the active outbreak and emerging spillover hypothesis — operational global-health context that bears on filovirus preparedness threads even when the digest is mostly small-molecule.
- [Zuckerman & Juavinett, *PLOS Comput Biol*, 2026-02-23](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1013970) — **Ten simple rules for building a collaborative coding culture.** Evidence-based recommendations distilled from 25+ researcher interviews on growing computational practice inside non-computational labs — directly relevant to Ersilia's capacity-building partnerships in Africa and to onboarding new Hub contributors.
- [Emma, *ChEMBL blog*, 2026-05-19](https://chembl.blogspot.com/2026/05/the-first-protac-has-been-approved-can.html) — **The first PROTAC has been approved: can we find it in ChEMBL?** ChEMBL is curating degrader–target interactions and introducing a MODALITY flag in v37 to surface PROTAC and molecular-glue chemistry — relevant infrastructure for the `glueAI` molecular-glue track and for any future Hub model on targeted protein degradation.
